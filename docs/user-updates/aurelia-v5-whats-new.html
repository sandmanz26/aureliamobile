What's new in Aurelia

You told us what you liked and what didn't feel right. This update is built around what you said: fewer numbers, more reasons, and an app that adapts to you.

How to try it: open Aurelia, tap Design at the top of the screen and choose v2. On the test site it is listed as v5. The changes are marked in orange on the screens below.

  • 1Progress in words, not scores
  • 2No more made-up numbers
  • 3A "Why?" on every suggestion
  • 4Aurelia plans your next chapter
  • 5Your wearable, asked up front
  • 6An optional journal
  • 7Community without competition
  • 8A clearer home page
1

Progress in words, not scores

The Progress tab now tells you how you've been feeling, in plain words, from your own check-ins. No percentages.

  1. 1A short summary of how you've felt so far, and the date of your next two-week report. Tap See what's behind this for each check-in.
  2. 2Your journal (see 6).
2

No more made-up numbers

We removed figures that weren't yours.

  1. AThe player shows the session itself, with no progress charts.
  2. BSession cards describe what the session is like, instead of claims such as "reduced stress by 43%". Tags no longer have a "#".
3

A "Why?" on every suggestion

When Aurelia suggests a change to your session, tap Why? to see what it does and what it is based on: your goal, how you felt last time, and your wearable if you connected one.

4

Aurelia plans your next chapter

After each session you tell Aurelia how you feel with one tap. It then tells you what it will change next time, so there is nothing for you to tune.

  1. 1What your wearable noticed during the session.
  2. 2An optional line for your journal.
  3. 3What the next chapter keeps and changes, with a reminder at the time you choose.

Wearable readings shown are examples until the connection goes live.

5

Your wearable, asked up front

During setup Aurelia asks if you wear an Apple Watch, Whoop or Oura Ring, and says exactly what it would read. Only the one you choose is switched on. "Not now" is always fine.

6

An optional journal

Write a short note after a session (see 4) or on any day from the Progress tab (see 1). Only you can see it, and Aurelia uses it to shape your next chapters.

7

Community without competition

  1. AChallenges are a shared journey: who joined and what they made, with no podium or rankings.
  2. BWhen you publish a session, Aurelia says plainly what others can see and what stays private: your check-ins, journal, wearable data and conversation.
8

A clearer home page

The first screen now says what Aurelia is: "Sessions made for how you feel today." The world map, which looked like a co-working space, is gone.

Thank you for the feedback. Keep it coming: tell us what works for you and what doesn't.
