Aurelia — Product Requirements (overview)

Status: Draft for review · Written: 11 Nov 2026 · Covers: production (prod v1 and prod v2), staging, and the admin site.

This is the whole product on one page: what Aurelia is, who it is for, what it does, what is built, what is not, how success is measured, and what has to be decided next. It is the overview. docs/PRD.md holds every detailed requirement and the reasoning behind each behaviour. The API contract (docs/api/API-CONTRACT.md) holds the backend plan.

Everything you can open today is a front-end product on sample data. No account persists, no real AI answers, and every figure (plays, "−43% stress", coins) is invented so the demo can be reasoned about. This document marks each feature as Built, Built on sample data, or Not built.

1 · Summary

Aurelia is a generative wellness companion. A person says how they feel or what they need, for example "I can't fall asleep, my mind keeps racing". Aurelia builds a personal audio-visual session for it: a guided script, a voice, a soundscape and visuals. They play it, tell Aurelia what to change, and come back to a journey of chapters that adapts to how they say they feel. Sessions can be published so others can play them or recreate their own version, with the original creator always credited.

The product's bet: a conversation is a better way in than a library. Someone who cannot sleep should not have to filter 40 meditations. They should say one sentence and be listening within a minute.

2 · The problem

What people told us What it means for the product
Choosing is hard when you already feel bad Minimal input. Aurelia decides first and the person removes what they do not want.
Generic content does not fit this feeling Every session is built for the sentence that started it.
Invented numbers are distrusted ("−43% stress?") No figure is shown that is not the person's own or that nobody measured.
Scores can make people anxious Progress is told in words, with a report every two weeks and detail on demand.
"Why is it suggesting this?" Every suggested change carries a reason (prod v2).
Competitive challenges feel wrong for well-being Challenges without ranks (prod v2).
Wearables are wanted, but with consent Asked once during onboarding, and off until switched on.

Sources: interviews with three real users (late October 2026), a simulation with six personas, and reviews by the product and business owners.

3 · Who it is for

The self-directed seeker (primary). They open the app when something is off: they cannot sleep, they are stressed before a meeting, their head is foggy. Success for them is the time from "Ask Aurelia…" to a session playing, and feeling it worked.

The community sharer (secondary). They have made sessions worth sharing and come back for recognition: plays, recreates, coins. Success for them is being recreated.

The operator (internal). The Aurelia team runs content, safety, AI cost, revenue and compliance from the admin site. Success for them is seeing a problem before a user reports it.

The researcher (internal, new). They run the 30-person feedback-group study of therapeutic outcomes, from the admin's Experimentation section.

Personas are inferred from the flows and the interviews. Confirm them with the business before launch.

4 · Product principles

These are decisions, not preferences. Each one has a reason recorded in docs/PRD.md or the work journal.

  1. The cockpit is the product. The chat is where sessions are made. Every other screen feeds it or shows what it made.
  2. Recommendations arrive applied. The person takes away what they do not want, rather than opting into each change.
  3. Honesty over impressiveness. A figure carries its provenance: self-reported, measured, or sample. Sample data is never presented as a person's own result.
  4. Privacy by default. Signal sources start off. Consent is stored per source. Publishing shares the session, never check-ins, journal, wearable data or the conversation.
  5. Attribution is not a toggle. A recreated session keeps its lineage, and the original creator stays credited.
  6. Safety runs on every word a person writes. A crisis signal goes to a person, not to an automated reply.
  7. A change makes a new version, and every version is kept. v1.0, v1.1, … Each can be played or restored.
  8. Staging first; production on request. Experiments ship as a switchable design version before they become anyone's default.

5.2 The core loop

Home: "Ask Aurelia…"  →  onboarding (goal, feeling, style; the sentence pre-fills it)
  →  the cockpit: Aurelia replies and proposes changes, already applied
  →  Apply  →  a build: v1.0, then v1.1, v1.2 … every version kept
  →  Play  →  "How do you feel now?" (1–5)  →  journal line (prod v2)
  →  "Chapter 2 is ready tomorrow"  →  calendar reminder
  →  Progress, told in words  →  back to the cockpit

5.5 Tools around the product

  • /__demo is a feature-flag console that scopes a walkthrough, with a "This build" panel naming the site, commit and build time. It is deliberately not linked from the app.
  • Review annotations: numbered pins on phone-width screens, shared per site.
  • Click insights: screens and taps per visit, labels and positions only, never field values.
  • The site password keeps strangers out of unfinished work. It is a courtesy, not security.

6 · AI

There is no model in the product yet. The cockpit's replies come from keyword rules, editable in the admin's Cockpit rules. The API contract specifies six AI capabilities, all server-side:

Capability What it does
Understanding the first sentence Turns "I can't sleep, my mind races" into a goal, a feeling and a starting style
The cockpit conversation Replies, asks when it must, and proposes changes
Proposals with a "Why" Each change says what it does and what it rests on (goal, last check-in, wearable)
The session build Script, then voice (text-to-speech), then sound, then visuals, as a job with streamed progress
The next-chapter plan What chapter N+1 changes, from the check-ins
Progress in words A plain-language summary of the person's own check-ins

A safety layer runs on every user-written input before generation. A crisis signal routes to crisis resources and to the on-call reviewer in Moderation. Quality is scored by an LLM-as-judge rubric on sampled traffic, and cost and latency are reported per route in AI monitoring.

7 · Data, privacy and safety

  • Special-category data. Mood, sleep, voice and biometrics are special-category data under GDPR Art. 9. Each purpose is consented separately and the consent text is versioned.
  • What publishing shares: title, sound, visuals and script. Never check-ins, the journal, wearable readings or the conversation.
  • Claims. Outcome figures are labelled self-reported on screen. Aurelia does not diagnose and does not claim to cause an improvement.
  • Well-being follow-up. A WHO-5 of 28 or below is a cue for a person to reach out, never an automated action.
  • Today: nothing leaves the browser except flags, annotations and click insights (labels and positions only).

8 · Business model

Not decided. The client's pricing is awaited.

  • A free limit exists in the product. It counts builds, not messages, and only after the first build. The number is a placeholder.
  • Plans: the admin's Pricing module shows a Free / Plus / Pro design. The API contract recommends 5 builds a day free; Plus 30 a day with 20-minute sessions and all voices; Pro unlimited within fair use.
  • Coins: one currency is recommended. Only one earning rule is defined today: the referral reward. That rule needs an activation trigger and a cap before launch, because sign-up alone is farmable.
  • Creators: when a recreated session earns coins, part of them follows the lineage chain. How far up the chain, and what share, is still open.

9 · How success is measured

Metric Definition Where it stands
Activation A new person plays a built session in their first visit Predicted 57% on v5 (simulation, not measured)
Retention Active on day 1 / day 7 / day 30 Predicted v5: 24% / 11.4% / 6.9%. Benchmark for wellness apps: 20–27% / 8–13% / 3–8%
Time to first play From the first sentence to audio playing Not instrumented yet
Therapeutic outcome WHO-5 change of 10 points or more; the condition measure reaching the healthy band The feedback-group study (30 people, 8 weeks) is designed but has not started
Share rate Recreates per published session Not instrumented yet

No retention number can be measured until sign-in persists. Every predicted figure comes from the persona simulation, adjusted with what the three interviews said. None of them is a measurement.

10 · Out of scope (for now)

  • Clinical treatment, diagnosis or a medical-device claim.
  • An editorial content library. The community is the catalogue.
  • Real-time group sessions.
  • A native wearable app. Signals come through Apple Health, Whoop and Oura integrations.

12 · Risks

Risk Why it matters Mitigation
A wellness claim without evidence Regulatory risk and loss of trust Provenance on every figure; the study before any outcome claim
A crisis message gets an automated answer Harm to a person A mandatory safety layer, a human on call, crisis resources per country
AI cost per build Margins on a free tier Per-route effort and model settings, evals before any downgrade, a spend ceiling in Settings
Voice quality The interviews named it A blind listening test of three text-to-speech vendors
The admin has no login Anyone with the address can change things Admin SSO with roles in P0
The demo looks real Promises about things that do not exist This document's status column; "sample data" labels in the app
