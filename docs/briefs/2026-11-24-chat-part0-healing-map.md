## 2. Tasks in this part, as we read them

1. **Make the icon correct.** Which icon, and correct in what way, is not stated. The only icon shown is his 2025 set and Aurelia mark. To confirm before any change.
2. **Use the five wikis as background.** The owner says this is already done.
3. **Retreat-in-a-Box's purpose, clarified.** It is a **reach engine, not a ticket business**:
   1. build reach through wellness influencers (retreat leaders);
   2. funnel participants into using Aurelia;
   3. convert them to paid plans inside the app.

   This agrees with the approved redesign, where Plus per ticket buyer is the number that decides the plan. His co-branded mock-up still carries a **physical box kit**; the redesign made the box a paid add-on.
4. **Google Analytics against our admin.** Research which of GA's most prominent features the admin is missing. New research task.
5. **Insight Timer quick wins.** Using the wiki's research and Product lab's data:
   - list the core features to replicate, adapted to our value and design;
   - then make an app version with them (on staging, as a new design version).
6. **A Healing Map.** Dynamic and interactive, as simple as Insight Timer's Map of Healing in the final output, but "more therapeutic" and built on our components and logic:
   - a Wellness State Graph that learns from results;
   - the Lego blocks;
   - the bad → technique → good geometry.

   Use the Living Intelligence tiles, the AKP graph, the State-Flip video and the AI vision as sources. Its job in his words: "validate the Math of Healing as medical foundation for our AI". The owner put it in the backlog.

## 3. First cautions (head of product)

- **Theirs is honest about what it shows; ours must be too.** Insight Timer's map states that a link is "a count of documented connections, never a claim of evidence strength", and it is built from 250,000 papers. A map that "learns from your results" needs real results we do not have yet. Until then it can show only sample or design data, labelled.
- **"Medical foundation" and "validate".** Validation needs studies and evidence. Calling it medical makes it a device claim. Internally we can call it a therapeutic model; publicly, wellness.
- **Invented units and figures** (TeraPhenoms, Q-flow, "+18% HRV", 120,000 elements, 1.5M links) are design fiction in investor visuals. Keep them out of any screen that reads as a measurement.
- **The game layer** (checkpoints, XP, "win your state flips") conflicts with earlier research that warns against streaks and punitive gamification (J094). A forgiving version (checkpoints, never streaks) can work. Decide deliberately.
- **Videos cannot be watched from here** (Vimeo). The Google Doc is the same as the PDF we have.
