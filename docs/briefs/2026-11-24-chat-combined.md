## 3. Cautions that apply across the tasks

- **Measured against assumed.** The only measured costs are the 2025 test sessions: €40.27 in total, voice 41%, video 37%. Everything about flips, chapters per flip, learning curves, density, velocity, 120,000 elements, 1.5M links and TeraPhenoms is a design assumption or investor visual. Reports label it so, or the business owner will be shown his own assumptions back as findings.
- **Medical wording.** Use "therapeutic" and "medical range" as internal metric names only. Public copy and partner material stay wellness. Real clinical claims are a separate decision (`/wiki/b2b2c/regulation`).
- **Price anchors disagree.** A flip at $19.99 (spec), Plus at $10 a month or $100 a year (the app), the retreat ticket at €19.99 or €39. The cost report shows cost per flip and leaves the revenue side switchable.
- **Feature lock (O54).** This chat adds about ten new tasks in one evening. The backend-facing parts (A's supplier routing, D's data, E's learning graph) wait for the v1 feature lock. The admin and wiki work can proceed on sample data.
