## 2. His thesis: "the wellness revenue model is a mathematical inevitability" (19.33–19.37)

**Table, airlines against Aurelia:**

| | Global airlines (2026) | Aurelia Meta-AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | 5.2B travellers | 500M humans in need |
| Annual "transfers" | ~10B journeys | 5B state-flips (10 per user) |
| Supply | Carriers (SAS, Norwegian), OTAs | AI models (LLM, LVM, LAM) |
| Logic layer | Momondo / Skyscanner | Aurelia Meta-AI Engine |
| Payoff | Certainty of arrival (physical) | "Certainty of Flip" (biological) |

**The argument:**
1. **Escaping the "static" trap.** Headspace and Calm are "FM radio in a Spotify world": 200M users, static libraries that do not intervene, a $5B market cap. Generic AI "hallucinates" wellness and lacks causality. Aurelia's edge is a Meta-AI Network, "our own momondo supply chain", where thousands of text, image, audio, voice and video models compete in internal tournaments, judged by the Causal AI, to deliver "the proven pathway to a state change".
2. **The economics of the state-flip.** There is a $100B+ market for vitamins and painkillers, bought for a guaranteed reaction. About 500M people want to move from "State X" (fog) to "State Y" (clarity); at 10 boosts a year that is 5 billion transfers. Buy "Noise" (compute) at commodity prices, sell "Signal" (the state-flip) as a premium utility. "As compute costs trend toward zero by 2028, our margins just keep expanding."
3. **Why not sell the engine (yet).** Selling to a legacy player is plan B. Aurelia is "the Logic Layer"; the models are the airlines. September 2006 (momondo's founding) "all over again", but in a "tsunamic" AGI race.
4. **The brand:** "the world's first meta-AI engine for your wellness quests", transparent, across all AI suppliers, free "to some degree". Users share "Insights" ("algorithmic AI token-compositions") so others can regenerate their state-flips, and earn AI credits for it.
5. **From Noise to Signal**, in four stages:
   1. **The Assembly**, a neural orchestration layer: a tournament across LLM, LVM and LAM models to find "your specific biological, psychological and relational factors".
   2. **The Morph**, a fidelity bridge: the complexity is hidden.
   3. **The Experience**, a cinematic AI stream that "breathes with you" and re-synthesises as you reflect.
   4. **The Output**, insights or legacy mapping: your evolution becomes "Chapters", proven pathways for the community.
6. **AI dominance.** By late 2026 over 90% of online content is AI-made, so "information" is a zero-cost commodity.
7. **The utility market.** An hour of lost productivity is worth $25–$150. The real value is "unpriced essentials": a restored night's sleep, a de-escalated conflict, the return of creative flow.
8. **The valuation.** "Even at a radical floor of $1.00 per flip." "We are purchasing 'noise' for pennies and selling 'certainty' for dollars."

## 7. What a head of product should not take at face value (first notes)

- **The spec's progression numbers are assumptions.** The 0.58x and 0.26x cost factors, the 54 → 89% flip rates, DCI and VCI, and 120,000 elements, 1.5M links and 100M pathways all need a source or a "target" label. The only measured costs are the 2025 test sessions in §3. The "Living Intelligence" tiles need the same treatment.
- **"Medical therapeutic" and "flip" claims** turn a wellness app into a medical device under the regulators' own tests (`/wiki/b2b2c/regulation`). Our internal metrics can measure state change; outside copy must say wellness. Clinical validation is a separate, deliberate track.
- **Prices disagree.** The spec prices a flip at $19.99 and subscriptions at $9.99 or $19.99. The app's Plus is $10 a month or $100 a year, and the retreat ticket is €19.99 or €39. The cost report must state which.
- **The 90-day MVP nets 4.8% only before fixed costs**, and the spec says so. The CAC of $15 per flip is assumed.
- **Video was 37% of 2025 cost**, and the spec drops it to "optional". This agrees with our Change Plan feedback: no video in the MVP.
- **Model tier.** He asks for "their best model" but agreed to start with the current one, then add the best tier to measure the uplift.
- **Words.** "Quantum superposition", "entanglement" and "Shannon-Neumann logic" (2025 vision) do not describe software we can build or test. Keep them out of the knowledge base as claims; keep the testable parts: the state graph, prompt iteration, the reward function and pruning.
