## What he wants, in one paragraph
A structured knowledge base the Causal AI navigates: **goals** (5 dimensions → universal needs → concrete bad-to-good StateFlips, 125 to start, 25 per dimension) linked to **strategies** (AKPs: concepts, skills, moods, methods, techniques, frameworks per modality) and to **how a session is assembled** (Meta → Path → Script, bars and elements). Its purpose is the product itself: making sessions and chapters that move real people from bad to good. The admin must show this system logic correctly; personas become "their own AI system" in the same dimensions; chapters, chance it works and cost must be explained from it.

## Plan (proposed, waiting for the product owner)
1. **Objective Learning: the 125 StateFlips.** 5 dimensions × 25. Each: universal need (from Deeksha UN and DOJO duals), bad state, good state, X → Y → Z milestones, how it is measured (check-in scale), who has it (persona v4), strategy AKPs that drive it (Grand 4 layers), chapters, chance it works and cost with formulas. Admin page in Product lab and a wiki tab. Expandable toward thousands.
2. **System logic in the admin.** The Meta → Path → Script cascade and AKP tournaments (AKPs for impact, AI models for quality and cost), drawn from BAR / ELEMENTS and the Notion backend overview, mapped to what our app and API contract do today.
3. **The chapter graph (cohort view).** For sleep first: how each chapter moves the score 25 → 85, which AKPs drive each step, and "chance it works" rebuilt as a transparent formula (stage, adherence, readiness, severity, fit) instead of the spec's flat 54%.
4. **Personas as their own AI system.** Persona v4 extended to the 12 Wellness Identity dimensions (Grand) and the Light sub-dimensions, joined to the condition model (U138).
5. **Then:** therapeutic funnel, cost per StateFlip wired into the Cost report, buyer test 8, Product and Business prediction (AI brain) and the weekly report.
