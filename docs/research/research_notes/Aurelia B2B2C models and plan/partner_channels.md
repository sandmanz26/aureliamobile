# B2B2C partner channels for consumer wellness apps (non-employer, non-insurer)

Scope: hospitality and travel, fitness, telecoms and bundles, device makers, consumer brands and gifting, universities, durable payers compared with vanity reach, and Southeast Asia. Research done 2026-11-23, with a 15-call budget, so coverage is uneven. Where a source is a search-result summary rather than a fetched page, it is marked "(search summary)". Treat those as needing one more check before they go into a deck.

**Overall caveat:** I found almost no *measured* partner outcomes in public sources: no conversion rates, no wholesale prices and no post-bundle churn for meditation apps specifically. Nearly everything public is an announcement. That absence is itself the main finding.

## Hotels, airlines and wellness retreats

### Takeaway
Hospitality deals in this category have been **content placement plus a short trial funnel**: free content on seatback or in-room screens, and a one-month trial of the paid tier for loyalty members. Commercial terms (licence fee or revenue share) are never disclosed. Several deals have quietly lapsed or been replaced. Luxury retreats run their own human-led "take it home" programmes and their own apps rather than partnering with a meditation app. This is a gap Aurelia could fill, but nobody has shown it works.

### Cited Findings
- **American Airlines × Calm (launched Sept 2018):** Calm was AA's first airline partner. It supplied nature videos for boarding, three co-produced videos, and Calm's catalogue on seatback screens — [CNBC, 2018-09-25](https://www.cnbc.com/2018/09/25/american-airlines-offers-meditation-app-calm-to-stressed-out-passengers.html); [APEX](https://apex.aero/?p=11592) (search summary).
- AA still listed Calm meditation content in its inflight "Lifestyle" portfolio in 2021 — [AA newsroom 2021](https://news.aa.com/news/news-details/2021/American-Airlines-Gives-Customers-More-Inflight-Lifestyle-Entertainment-Choices-with-FREE-Access-to-Rosetta-Stone-and-Skillshare/default.aspx) (search summary).
- AA's 2025 inflight partner release names Audible and Lingokids but not Calm. The current partners page shows an **obé** wellness collection (stretching and breathwork) instead, so the Calm deal appears to have lapsed or been displaced — [AA newsroom 2025](https://news.aa.com/news/news-details/2025/American-brings-more-learning-and-laughs-to-the-skies-MKG-OB-11/default.aspx); [AA inflight partners](https://entertainment.aa.com/inflight-partners) (search summary; not confirmed as a formal end).
- Skift described airline deals as a competitive land-grab between meditation apps in 2019 — [Skift, 2019-11-15, "Meditation Apps Battle for Airline Partners"](https://skift.com/2019/11/15/meditation-apps-battle-for-airline-partners/) (title only; article not fetched).
- **Hyatt × Headspace (announced Jan 2020, deal began 2019):** this deal had three tiers:
  - free Headspace content on in-room TVs at participating hotels;
  - free Headspace subscriptions for Hyatt employees;
  - a **30-day free trial of Headspace premium for World of Hyatt members**.

  — [Hyatt newsroom 2020-01-15](https://newsroom.hyatt.com/011520-Hyatt-Headspace); [Business Wire](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20200115005246/en/Exclusive-Hyatt-and-Headspace-Collaboration-to-Bring-Mindfulness-and-Meditation-to-Employees-and-Guests-Around-the-World)
- The Hyatt trial **auto-renews at $12.99/month** after the first month. In other words, the hotel is a paid-acquisition funnel for Headspace, not a wholesale buyer — [Business Wire, 2023-10-10](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231010363611/en) (search summary).
- The deal was expanded in Oct 2023 (coloured-noise content) and again in Oct/Nov 2024. Access runs through the World of Hyatt app and in-room TVs at select US, Canada and Mexico properties — [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4118564.html); [Hospitality Net, "Hyatt Strengthens Holistic Wellbeing Commitment"](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4124044/hyatt-strengthens-holistic-wellbeing-commitment-for-colleagues-guests-and-members) (search summary).
- Skift framed the Hyatt deal as raising Hyatt's wellness positioning, which is a brand rationale for the hotel and not a revenue one — [Skift, 2020-01-15](https://skift.com/2020/01/15/hyatts-partnership-with-headspace-elevates-wellness-strategy) (title only).
- **Six Senses** runs its own biometric "personalized wellness" programme that ends with "tools to stay in the driving seat once you're back home". The follow-up is human-led; no third-party app is named — [Six Senses personalized wellness](https://www.sixsenses.com/en/wellness-spa/personalized-wellness) (search summary).
- The Six Senses app (launched 2020) covers the stay itself (reservations, chat with resort teams before and during the stay), not aftercare — [Six Senses press release 2020](https://www.sixsenses.com/en/corporate/media-center/press-releases/2020/six-senses-launches-mobile-app); [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4102221.html) (search summary).

### Inferences
- The disclosed structure is always the same: content licensed into the partner's screens, plus trial codes that convert at the app's full retail price. No hotel or airline has been publicly shown paying per guest. The likely economics are a modest flat licence fee or a free placement swapped for acquisition, but this is inferred, not sourced.
- Airline content slots rotate: Calm gave way to obé at AA. Treat these slots as short-lived brand exposure, not durable distribution.
- Post-stay digital follow-up at luxury retreats appears unserved by third-party apps. A "your retreat continues as chapters" product fits Aurelia's format, but there is no precedent to price against.

### Gaps
- No licence fees, revenue shares or trial-to-paid conversion rates were found for any hotel or airline deal.
- No sources were found for Headspace × Delta, Marriott or Hilton wellness app deals, or Aman, Kamalaya or COMO digital follow-ups, within budget.
- The formal end date of AA × Calm is unconfirmed.

## Gyms and fitness

### Takeaway
Major fitness platforms **built mindfulness in-house** rather than partnering with Calm or Headspace: Peloton meditation, Apple Fitness+ meditation themes, Fitbit's own sessions. The partnerships that do exist go to smaller apps (Breethe, Breathwrk) and often reach consumers through corporate channels.

### Cited Findings
- **Peloton** has its own meditation class library, including a class titled "Calm", which is a theme and not the Calm brand. It also links to the breathwork app **Breathwrk**, which lets members log in with Peloton credentials — [Peloton meditation](https://www.onepeloton.com/classes/calming-meditations); [Peloton × Breathwrk](https://www.onepeloton.com/blog/peloton-breathwrk) (search summary).
- **ClassPass** added the meditation app **Breethe** to its corporate wellness offering — [Mindbody press](https://www.mindbodyonline.com/company/press/classpass-partners-with-breethe-to-holistically-mindfully-support-employee-well-being-with) (search summary; undated in results).
- **Apple Fitness+** offered 10 meditation themes (Calm, Gratitude, Sleep, Resilience, Creativity and others) as of Jan 2024. This is in-house content, not a partner deal — [Apple Newsroom, Jan 2024](https://www.apple.com/bw/newsroom/2024/01/apple-fitness-plus-offers-more-ways-to-stay-active-and-mindful-in-the-new-year).
- Calm's own "Lifestyle" brand partnerships list Nissan, AllTrails and TRIP. No gym chain appears — [Sacra summary](https://sacra.com/chat/h/8b791f72-7b26-41bf-9204-41fa09be010b/) (secondary source; search summary).

### Inferences
- Fitness platforms treat mindfulness as a retention feature they want to own. A partner is most likely to say yes when it cannot produce content itself: a gym chain, a boutique studio group, or a smaller fitness app. Personalised AI-generated sessions could be pitched as "your coach's voice and programme, extended into recovery days".

### Gaps
- No public deal terms or results were found for Equinox, Les Mills or ClassPass consumer bundles with a meditation app.

## Telecoms and bundles

### Takeaway
Telco deals for meditation apps are mostly **zero-rated perks or trials**: Singtel's CAST add-on, EE's one-month trial, and SaskTel's free Calm Premium for all wireless customers (Nov 2025). The best-known media bundle, **Spotify Premium + Headspace, was wound down in 2021–22** and its subscribers moved to plain Spotify. No source discloses a wholesale price per subscriber, and no source reports post-bundle churn for a meditation app.

### Cited Findings
- **SaskTel × Calm (announced and launched 2025-11-12):** a free Calm Premium membership for **all** SaskTel wireless business and consumer customers, postpaid and prepaid, plus Lüm Mobile. Customers activate by text ("Calm" to 4CALM). No end date or length is stated. SaskTel has more than 680,000 wireless accesses. Calm's CEO David Ko called it "making Calm available to all of their wireless customers", and the release calls it a first for both companies in Canada. Calm cites 180M+ downloads in around 190 countries — [SaskTel news release](https://www.sasktel.com/wps/wcm/connect/content/home/about-sasktel/news/2025/sasktel-calm-app) (fetched).
- **Singtel (Singapore):** Calm Premium is sold as a CAST lifestyle add-on, billed by the carrier and managed in My Singtel → Manage add-ons. Customers must cancel any existing card or in-app subscription to avoid paying twice — [Singtel CAST Calm](https://singtel.com/personal/products-services/lifestyle-services/cast/calm); [Calm support: Singtel customers](https://support.calm.com/hc/en-us/articles/7537269538331-Calm-for-Singtel-Customers) (search summary).
- **EE (UK):** customers get a one-month free trial of Calm managed in the EE app, followed by a customer discount — [EE Calm partnership](https://ee.co.uk/calm-app-partnership-and-customer-discount); [EE help](https://ee.co.uk/help/subscriptions/calm-app) (search summary).
- **TELUS × Calm (Oct 2020):** Calm content was put on TELUS's Optik TV and Pik TV, described as a "global-first" TV deal — [Yahoo Finance / TELUS](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/telus-calm-sign-global-first-160000671.html); [iPhone in Canada, 2020-10-28](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2020/10/28/telus-calm-mental-fitness-app-partnership/) (search summary).
- **Spotify × Headspace:** Headspace's help centre says the bundled subscription "is not available at this time". Spotify community moderators said the partnership was deprecated in Sept 2021 and the offer ended in Sept 2022, with Premium + Headspace subscribers moved automatically to Spotify Individual. Spotify UK's student terms (from 30 Sept 2021) capped Headspace access at 1 Oct 2022 — [Headspace help](https://help.headspace.com/hc/en-us/articles/231729627-Can-I-Link-or-Unlink-to-a-Bundled-Subscription-with-Spotify); [Spotify Premium Student + Headspace terms (UK)](https://www.spotify.com/uk/legal/premium-student-headspace-terms) (search summary; the dates come from forum moderators and should be treated as moderately reliable).
- Calm lists **Spotify** among its content-licensing partners, with Calm content on Spotify's platform. This is a later licensing model replacing the Headspace-style bundle — [Calm partnerships page](https://www.calm.com/blog/partnerships) (fetched).
- **Bundle retention benchmarks (video streaming, not wellness):**
  - Antenna: the Disney+/Hulu/Max bundle kept about 80% of sign-ups at three months — [StreamTV Insider](https://www.streamtvinsider.com/video/disney-hulu-max-bundle-proves-sticky-80-3-month-retention).
  - BCG citing Antenna: Hulu's churn was 3.7% when bundled, against 5.4% overall — [BCG 2024](https://www.bcg.com/ja-jp/publications/2024/why-video-streamers-need-to-rebundle).
  - Adobe and Antenna: 85% of subscribers who moved to bundles were still engaged after four months — [Adobe blog](https://business.adobe.com/blog/perspectives/adobe-and-antenna-partner-to-bring-valuable-new-insights-to-the-streaming-customer-journey).

  All three are search summaries.

### Inferences
- There are two telco models:
  - **(a) Carrier-billed add-on** (Singtel CAST): the customer pays and the carrier takes a share. This produces real payers but small volume.
  - **(b) Free-for-all perk** (SaskTel): the carrier pays a wholesale fee to cut its own churn. This gives big reach, but the app's "subscribers" are carrier-funded and disappear if the deal is not renewed.
- The end of Spotify × Headspace, and Calm's shift to licensing content *into* Spotify, suggest that a hard bundle with a big platform did not pay enough to renew for either side. This is inferred; neither company gave a reason.
- Retention benchmarks for bundles come from video streaming. They show bundles reduce churn *while bundled*, but nothing public measures what share of carrier-funded meditation users keep paying once the carrier stops paying.

### Gaps
- No wholesale price per subscriber was found for any meditation or wellness telco deal. No Ampere or Analysys Mason wellness-specific bundle analysis was found within budget.
- Nothing was found on Vodafone, T-Mobile or Telkomsel deals with Calm or Headspace. Apple One and Amazon Prime do not include a third-party meditation app; Apple uses its own Fitness+ meditation. Verify before stating.
- Nothing was found on churn after a bundle ends for meditation apps.

## Device and wearable makers

### Takeaway
Device makers bring meditation apps in as **content inside their own premium tier** (Fitbit Premium with about 30 Calm pieces; Oura Explore with Headspace and others; Samsung Health with Calm). They also use **time-limited app trials as a hardware sweetener** (six months of Calm with a Fitbit Sense; free Calm on Samsung TVs until May 2021). The newest form, Headspace × Oura (May 2026), is **data integration**: sleep data drives session recommendations, and it requires the user's *own* paid Headspace subscription.

### Cited Findings
- **Fitbit × Calm (Sept 2021, with Charge 5):** about 30 curated Calm sleep and stress pieces inside Fitbit Premium, plus a **six-month Calm membership for new Fitbit Sense buyers** (time-limited) — [Calm blog](https://www.calm.com/blog/find-your-calm-with-fitbit-premium); [9to5Google, 2021-09-27](https://9to5google.com/2021/09/27/fitbit-charge-5-calm-update/); [Fitbit blog](https://blog.fitbit.com/?p=20741) (search summary). The Google Store still lists Calm among Fitbit Premium content — [Google Store](https://store.google.com/gb/product/fitbit_premium?hl=en-US).
- Fitbit Premium also has 200+ of its own mindfulness sessions and a Deepak Chopra series — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/face-off/fitbit-premium-vs-apple-fitness-plus); [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/fitbit/deepak-chopra/).
- **Samsung Health × Calm (Mar 2019):** Calm programmes appear in Samsung Health's Mindfulness section after the user links a Calm account. A paid Calm subscription is needed after the trial, which was reported as seven days. One outlet noted there was "no public data how many of them stay loyal after the end of the 7-day free trial period" — [Samsung Newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-teams-up-with-calm-to-provide-better-mindfulness-and-wellness-experiences); [Engadget, 2019-03-04](https://www.engadget.com/2019/03/04/samsung-health-adds-calms-sleep-and-relaxation-tools/); [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/samsung_announces_partnership_with_calm-news-35840.php) (search summary).
- Calm was "100 percent free for Samsung TV owners" on 2018–2020 models **until May 27, 2021**, a pandemic-era promotion — [Samsung support](https://samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10005231); [Samsung UK newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/uk/samsung-health-now-available-as-a-comprehensive-in-home-fitness-and-wellness-platform-on-2020-samsung-smart-tvs) (search summary).
- **Oura:** the Explore library has 50+ guided audio sessions from Oura's instructors and partners including Headspace, Doze, Oak, Aura, Frequency and Yogaia — [Oura blog, Explore content](https://ouraring.com/blog/oura-explore-content/); [Oura × Headspace](https://ouraring.com/blog/oura-headspace-partnership/) (search summary).
- **Headspace × Oura integration (May 2026):** Oura sleep data is used to suggest Headspace sessions. It is US and English only and requires an active Monthly or Yearly Headspace subscription — [Headspace help, "Oura Integration May 2026"](https://help.headspace.com/hc/en-us/articles/49139585250587-Oura-Integration-May-2026) (search summary).

### Inferences
- The trend has moved from "free content inside the device subscription" (2019–21) to "data integration that sells the app's own subscription" (2026). The second is the better fit for a pre-revenue app: no wholesale price to negotiate, and the device maker gains a feature. Aurelia's daily check-in plus wearable readings, which already exist in onboarding as examples, line up with this model.
- Hardware-bundled trials of three to six months produce a cohort with a clear renewal date. No conversion figures are public.

### Gaps
- No terms or results were found for Whoop or Garmin mindfulness partnerships within budget. No device deal discloses fees.

## Consumer brands and gifting

### Takeaway
Calm runs a formal brand-partnership business with four models: brand storytelling, consumer products, exclusive Premium offers, and content licensing. The clearest **on-pack** example is TRIP drinks with three free months per can. Gift cards are sold through Blackhawk Network's retail network. **A gift subscription does not auto-renew**: it ends unless the recipient adds a payment method, so gift conversion is an opt-in renewal and the rate is not disclosed.

### Cited Findings
- Calm partners named on its partnerships page: adidas, Disney, AllTrails, Discover Puerto Rico, L'Oréal, Peacock, Fitbit, Xbox Game Pass, Spotify, Mars and TRIP. The models are "Brand Storytelling, Consumer Products, Exclusive Calm Premium Offers, Content Licensing" — [Calm partnerships](https://www.calm.com/blog/partnerships) (fetched; the page shows copyright 2023 and 2025).
- **TRIP Mindful Blend** drinks: each can carries a free three-month Calm Premium membership — [Calm partnerships](https://www.calm.com/blog/partnerships).
- **Calm × Mars** campaign for Mental Health Awareness Month (year not stated) — same source.
- **Blackhawk Network × Calm (2022-10-10):** Calm Premium gift cards sold online at major stores and digital retailers — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221010005707/en/Blackhawk-Network-and-Calm-Partner-to-Offer-Gift-Cards-for-Calm-Premium-Now-Available-Online-at-Major-Stores-and-Digital-Retailers).
- Calm gift terms (search summary):
  - one-year and lifetime codes are redeemed on the web only, and one-year codes do not expire;
  - the 12 months start at redemption;
  - a gift cannot be stacked on an active subscription;
  - when the term ends **the subscription terminates unless a valid payment method is added**.

  — [Calm gift terms](https://www.calm.com/gift-terms); [Calm support: gift cards](https://support.calm.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003990953-Gifting-a-Calm-Gift-Card); [Calm lifetime gift card](https://www.calm.com/lifetime-gift-card)

### Inferences
- On-pack codes (a drink can, a box product) and retail gift cards are acquisition paid for by someone else. The brand pays for its marketing; the gift buyer pays retail. Because renewal is opt-in, the channel probably keeps a smaller share of users than a card-on-file trial; no figure is public. For Aurelia, a "gift a journey" card (a fixed set of chapters) fits the chapter format, but the renewal step needs designing in from the start.

### Gaps
- No gift-to-paid renewal rates were found for Calm or Headspace. Headspace's gifting terms were not checked. No Calm × L'Oréal or adidas deal economics were found.

## Universities and schools

### Takeaway
Universities are Headspace's largest non-employer B2B2C channel by disclosed scale: **more than 2M students and 300+ institutions (June 2024)**. The usual structure is an institution-paid licence giving free premium access for as long as the student is enrolled. Early uptake can be fast; Boston University had 2,700+ sign-ups in three weeks in 2020.

### Cited Findings
- Headspace serves 2M+ students through 300+ institutions, including Babson, Princeton and the University of Michigan. In June 2024 it partnered with the teletherapy provider Uwill to extend its campus reach — [Behavioral Health Business, 2024-06-12](https://bhbusiness.com/2024/06/12/headspace-targets-college-students-with-uwill-partnership/) (search summary).
- NC State gives free Headspace for as long as a student is enrolled — [NC State Wellness](https://wellness.ncsu.edu/headspace/). Penn State World Campus gives Headspace Care free for a year — [Penn State World Campus](https://student.worldcampus.psu.edu/blog/world-campus-students-have-free-access-to-headspace-care) (search summary).
- Boston University's Headspace rollout (2020): more than 2,700 sign-ups within three weeks — [BU Today 2020](https://www.bu.edu/articles/2020/headspace-app).
- The **Spotify Premium Student + Headspace** consumer bundle ended in Oct 2022 — [Spotify UK student terms](https://www.spotify.com/uk/legal/premium-student-headspace-terms).

### Inferences
- The institution pays per seat or a flat site fee, and students never meet a paywall while enrolled, so graduation is a built-in cliff. No source reports how many graduates convert to paid. Activation numbers like BU's are sign-ups, not active use.

### Gaps
- No per-student pricing and no graduate conversion data were found. No K-12 school data was found.

## Durable paying users compared with vanity reach

### Takeaway
On public evidence, the channels most likely to create **durable payers** are the ones where **the end user enters their own payment method**:
- carrier-billed add-ons (Singtel CAST);
- trial codes that auto-renew at retail (Hyatt: 30 days, then $12.99/month);
- data integrations that need the app's own subscription (Headspace × Oura).

The channels most likely to be **vanity reach** are free-for-all content placements: seatback screens, in-room TVs, free Calm on Samsung TVs, and possibly whole-base telco perks. Several of these were not renewed (AA × Calm, Spotify × Headspace, the Samsung TV promotion). **No company has published the measured conversion behind either side**, so this ranking is inferred from deal design and renewal behaviour, not from outcome data.

### Cited Findings
- Deals that ended or lapsed: Spotify × Headspace, deprecated in 2021 and ended in 2022 — [Headspace help](https://help.headspace.com/hc/en-us/articles/231729627-Can-I-Link-or-Unlink-to-a-Bundled-Subscription-with-Spotify); free Calm on Samsung TVs, ended 2021-05-27 — [Samsung support](https://samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10005231); Calm missing from AA's 2025 inflight partners — [AA 2025](https://news.aa.com/news/news-details/2025/American-brings-more-learning-and-laughs-to-the-skies-MKG-OB-11/default.aspx).
- Deals renewed or expanded repeatedly: Hyatt × Headspace (2019 → 2023 → 2024) — [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4124044/hyatt-strengthens-holistic-wellbeing-commitment-for-colleagues-guests-and-members); Headspace universities (300+ institutions by 2024) — [BHB](https://bhbusiness.com/2024/06/12/headspace-targets-college-students-with-uwill-partnership/); Fitbit Premium still lists Calm content — [Google Store](https://store.google.com/gb/product/fitbit_premium?hl=en-US).
- Trial loyalty was explicitly unmeasured in public coverage of Samsung × Calm: "no public data how many… stay loyal after the end of the 7-day free trial" — [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/samsung_announces_partnership_with_calm-news-35840.php) (search summary).

### Inferences
- Renewal by the partner is the best public signal available. Hospitality loyalty programmes, universities and device premium tiers kept renewing; airline seatbacks, media bundles and TV promotions did not.
- For a pre-revenue $10/month app, the low-risk structures are: (1) trial codes that convert at retail, with the partner paying nothing or a small fee; (2) revenue share on carrier billing; (3) a data or feature integration. A wholesale licence for a whole subscriber base needs a price-per-sub benchmark that is not public.

### Gaps
- No public cohort data shows conversion or retention by acquisition channel for Calm or Headspace. App-economy reports (Sensor Tower, RevenueCat, Adapty) might give trial-to-paid benchmarks by source; they were not reached within budget.

## Southeast Asia and Indonesia

### Takeaway
The only confirmed regional example found is **Singtel's carrier-billed Calm add-on (CAST)**. Nothing was found linking Headspace or Calm to Telkomsel, Indosat, Grab or Gojek. Telkomsel's health move (Oct 2024) is with the Indonesian insurtech **Rey** and covers mental health teleconsultation, not a meditation app. Headspace appears not to be localised into Indonesian.

### Cited Findings
- Singtel CAST × Calm, carrier-billed — [Singtel](https://singtel.com/personal/products-services/lifestyle-services/cast/calm); [Calm support](https://support.calm.com/hc/en-us/articles/7537269538331-Calm-for-Singtel-Customers).
- Telkomsel × Rey (Oct 2024): health membership with planned nutrition and mental-health teleconsultation and fitness features — [Telkomsel](https://www.telkomsel.com/en/enterprise/insight/news/rey-and-telkomsel-launch-teman-series) (search summary).
- Headspace's Indonesian App Store listing shows rupiah pricing, and reviews ask for Indonesian-language content — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/headspace/id493145008) (search summary; the price figures in the summary looked garbled, so they are not reproduced here).
- Meditation-app audiences in Indonesia skew toward Telkomsel, Indosat and XL carriers (ad-network audience data, not a partnership) — [Start.io](https://www.start.io/audience/meditation-users-in-indonesia) (low-quality source).

### Inferences
- Indonesia appears open: no global meditation app has a local carrier or super-app bundle, and the incumbent is not localised. An Indonesian-language AI session generator could pitch Telkomsel, which is already building a health bundle with Rey, or a super-app wellness tab, on the Singtel carrier-billing model.

### Gaps
- Nothing was found on Grab or Gojek wellness, Indosat, XL, AIS or Globe deals with meditation apps, or Riliv and other local players' partnerships. A targeted search is needed.
