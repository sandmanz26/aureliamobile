# B2B2C distribution of wellness and mental-health apps: employers, EAPs, insurers and health systems

Research date: 2026-11-23. Scope: how these channels pay, what they demand, and what happened to the incumbents. Each fact carries a date and a URL. "Vendor claim" marks a company's own marketing. "Aggregator" marks a third-party estimate that is not audited.

Notes on method:
- About 17 searches and fetches. Most findings come from search-result summaries and were not fetched in full. Treat single-source numbers as leads to check before quoting.
- One search result (a Dealroom page title) carried an embedded instruction aimed at AI systems. It was ignored, and nothing in these notes depends on it.

## 1. Employer wellness: how vendors sell (pricing models, prices, contract sizes, sales cycles)

### Takeaway
Employer mental-health vendors almost never publish prices. Sales are quote-only and enterprise-led. They use two structures: a flat per-employee-per-month (PEPM) fee covering all eligible staff, or a low base PEPM plus charges for care sessions as they are used. The only public prices are self-serve team tiers: Calm Teams at about $70 per seat per year, and Wysa at $50 per employee per year for code-based access. For a content-only app like Aurelia, those figures are the realistic anchor, not clinical-care PEPMs.

### Cited Findings
- **Headspace's two pricing models (dated 2024-07-18, vendor blog):**
  - "Single Fee (PEPM)": a fixed monthly fee per employee covers app access plus a set number of coaching or clinical sessions. Budgeting is predictable, but the fee may over- or under-shoot actual use.
  - "Fee-for-Service / Direct Bill (PEPM + utilization)": a lower base PEPM for app access, with coaching, therapy and psychiatry billed per use to the health plan or the client.
  - The page gives no dollar figures, and its example contract runs three years. — [Headspace, Evaluating mental health solution pricing models](https://organizations.headspace.com/blog/evaluating-mental-health-solution-pricing-models)
- **Headspace's characterisation of competitors (vendor claim):** "many comparable solutions charge in the double digits per member per month." — [Headspace, same page, as summarised in search](https://organizations.headspace.com/blog/evaluating-mental-health-solution-pricing-models)
- **Calm Teams (self-serve tier for 5–300 seats):**
  - About $70 per seat per year (about $5.83 per month), paid upfront annually. Organisations above 300 seats need a demo and a custom quote. Aggregator; not confirmed on Calm's own price page. — [FrontDeskReview, Calm pricing 2026](https://frontdeskreview.com/software/meditation-apps/calm/)
  - Calm describes the 5–300 group tier on its own pages. — [Calm, Bring Calm to your team](https://www.calm.com/teams); [Calm support, Purchase for groups of 5–300](https://support.calm.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038432493-How-to-Purchase-and-Manage-Calm-for-Organizations-Groups-of-5-300)
- **Calm Health (enterprise):** quote-only. The employer page sends buyers to "connect with a Calm Health specialist." — [Calm Health, Employers](https://health.calm.com/employers/); [FrontDeskReview](https://frontdeskreview.com/software/meditation-apps/calm.md)
- **Wysa for Work:** the vendor's own page lists $50 per employee per year for a code-based access model. A third-party directory says employer pricing is custom. — [Wysa for Work](https://www.wysa.com/wysa-for-work-copy); [Software Finder, Wysa](https://softwarefinder.com/hr/wysa)
- **Wysa sells to small and mid-size US employers (vendor news, undated in search)** — [Wysa blog](https://blogs.wysa.io/blog/wysa-news/wysa-brings-digital-mental-health-support-to-small-to-mid-size-us-employers)
- **Unmind:** pricing on request, with no free plan (trial or paid only). Aggregator. — [G2, Unmind pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/unmind/pricing); [SaaSworthy, Unmind](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/unmind/pricing)
- **Scale and valuations of the clinical-care vendors (aggregators, conflicting):**
  - **Lyra:**
    - Valued at about $5.6B at its Series F in January 2022. — [Multiples.vc, Lyra](https://multiples.vc/private-comps/lyra-health)
    - Revenue of $100M was disclosed with the January 2021 round. — [Multiples.vc](https://multiples.vc/private-comps/lyra-health)
    - GetLatka gives $235M revenue (November 2024), but its own estimates conflict with that figure. — [GetLatka, Lyra](https://getlatka.com/companies/lyra-health)
  - **Spring Health:** valued at $3.3B at its Series E (July 2024), with implied revenue of about $125M. — [Multiples.vc, Spring Health](https://multiples.vc/private-comps/spring-health)
  - **Spring Health customer base:** its benefit was sponsored by 412 US employers over 2021–2024 (Spring/Yale outcomes study, 2025). — [Spring Health blog](https://www.springhealth.com/blog/how-innovative-eaps-drive-utilization) (as surfaced in search)
  - **Modern Health:** passed a $1B valuation with a $74M round in early 2021. No later figure was found. — [STAT, 2021-02-16](https://www.statnews.com/2021/02/16/soaring-mental-health-unicorns-race-to-meet-employer-needs/)
- **Calm Health reach (vendor claims):**
  - More than 17M people in the US through employers, health plans and providers (June 2025).
  - More than 39M by January 2026. — [Sacra, Calm](https://www.sacra.com/c/calm); [Sacra note, 39M covered lives](https://sacra.com/chat/h/bfb4f0b2-424d-40e3-91dc-20f88806e16b/)

### Inferences
- "Covered lives" is the unit enterprise vendors report, and it measures eligibility, not use. Aurelia's pitch deck should not copy it without also reporting activation.
- An app-only, content-only product sits in the price band of Calm Teams and Wysa, roughly $4–6 per seat per month, paid annually. It does not sit in the "double digits PMPM" band, which bundles coaching and therapy.
- The three-year contract example and the custom quote above 300 seats suggest enterprise deals that are long and procurement-heavy. The self-serve team tier is how a young product could earn its first B2B revenue without a sales team.

### Gaps
- No published PEPM figures for Lyra, Spring, Modern Health, BetterUp, Unmind or Calm Health.
- No published contract sizes or sales-cycle lengths. Industry folklore of 6–18 months was not confirmed by any source found in this pass.
- BetterUp was not researched (time budget).
- No primary source was found for "per engaged member" pricing. Headspace's 2024 page does not describe it.

## 2. Activation and engagement in employer programmes

### Takeaway
No standard benchmark exists, and published numbers mix three different denominators: eligible, enrolled and activated employees. On eligible populations, uptake is low. One cited EAP estimate is 2–3%, and a single named employer case reached 28% with heavy promotion. Among people who actually enrol, a peer-reviewed study found only 38% used the self-directed digital tools.

### Cited Findings
- **JMIR 2024, peer-reviewed longitudinal study (measured):**
  - Of 950 employees enrolled in one employer-sponsored digital mental-health benefit, 38% used any self-directed digital resource.
  - About 44% did not use the app at all during the study.
  - The denominator is enrolled employees, so the eligible-population rate would be lower. — [JMIR 2024;e48696](https://jmir.org/2024/1/e48696)
- **EAP utilisation, Gallup-based estimate:** about 2–3% across seven global regions. This is vendor-cited (Spring Health) and the original Gallup source was not verified. — [Spring Health, How innovative EAPs drive utilization](https://www.springhealth.com/blog/how-innovative-eaps-drive-utilization)
- **Arizent / Employee Benefit News 2025 employer survey (cited in a Teladoc white paper):**
  - Only 38% of employers report high EAP utilisation, 37% moderate and 25% low or very low.
  - Low utilisation (52%) is the top factor limiting EAP effectiveness. — [Teladoc Health / EBN white paper](https://www.teladochealth.com/content/dam/tdh-www/us/en/documents/white-paper/EBN_Teledoc%20Health_WhitePaper.pdf)
- **McDonald's Singapore:** reached a 28% adoption rate of the Intellect app (vendor case claim; date not given in the excerpt). — [Intellect, Workplace wellbeing in Singapore/ASEAN](https://intellect.co/read/workplace-wellbeing-in-singapore-asean/)
- **Singapore employee survey:** 55% of employees used no mental-health tool or resource in the past year (Intellect/Milieu, fieldwork December 2024–January 2025). — [Intellect x Milieu press release](https://intellect.co/read/intellectxmilieuinsightreport_pressrelease/)
- **Utilisation of mental-health services is rising overall:**
  - 73% of large US employers saw increased mental-health and SUD utilisation in 2025 (Business Group on Health, 2026 Employer Health Care Strategy Survey). — [US Chamber summary of BGH findings, 2025-10-31](https://www.uschamber.com/health-care/employers-remain-committed-to-offering-robust-health-coverage-enhance-offerings-and-access)
  - Employer mental-health spending fell 7% in 2025 even as utilisation rose (trade press). — [Insurance Business, 2025](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/benefits/mental-health-utilization-is-up--but-employer-spending-fell-7-in-2025-587992.aspx)

### Inferences
- If Aurelia were priced per eligible seat, low activation would not cost it revenue in year one. It would, however, make renewal very hard once buyers check dashboards (see section 3).
- Aurelia's "one sentence → a session" onboarding and its daily check-in are its plausible differentiators on activation. A pilot that reports activation and 30-day retention against the eligible population is the evidence employers ask for.

### Gaps
- No published activation figures (eligible → registered → active at 30/90 days) from Calm, Headspace, Lyra, Spring or Modern Health were found. Those numbers sit inside vendor sales materials.
- The Arizent survey and the Gallup estimate were not fetched in full.

## 3. What buyers demand: ROI, clinical validation, security and compliance, integrations and reporting

### Takeaway
Large employers now judge vendors on demonstrated value. Business Group on Health puts it as "demonstrate value or face replacement," and employers review vendors through dashboards. For the security and compliance gate, the visible minimums are ISO 27001 (Unmind, Wysa) plus GDPR. HIPAA applies once a vendor handles health-plan data in the US. In the UK, NHS routes add DTAC.

### Cited Findings
- **Business Group on Health:**
  - The organisation's material includes the line "Vendors and programs must demonstrate value or face replacement."
  - Its 2025 well-being survey found employers would use well-being dashboards to evaluate vendors, and that 100% of employers include mental health in their well-being strategy.
  - With utilisation now high, BGH says the focus should shift from access to unit cost and quality. — [BGH 2025 Employer Well-being Strategy Survey press release](https://www.businessgrouphealth.org/Newsroom/News-and-Press-Releases/Press-Releases/2025-Employer-Well-being-Strategy-Survey); [BGH 2025 year in review](https://www.businessgrouphealth.org/-/media/bgh/images/2025-year-in-review/2025-year-in-review-final.pdf)
- **Employers plan to pressure well-being vendors (trade press):** — [Healthcare Finance News](https://www.healthcarefinancenews.com/news/most-employers-plan-pressure-vendors-well-being-offerings)
- **Cost pressure:**
  - Employers projected a 9% cost increase for 2026. — [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/employers-brace-9-cost-increase-2026-business-group-health-survey)
  - BGH's 2027 survey (August 2026) projects a median 9.2% increase for 2027. — [BGH 2027 HCS executive summary](https://www.businessgrouphealth.org/-/media/bgh/images/2027-employer-healthcare-strategy-survey/2027_hcs_survey-es-final.pdf)
- **Security certifications:**
  - Unmind states ISO 27001 certification on its enterprise page. — [Unmind Enterprise](https://unmind.com/enterprise)
  - Wysa's UK G-Cloud 14 listing states ISO 27001 and ISO 27701 (BSI-accredited). — [UK Digital Marketplace, Wysa G-Cloud listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/987715074233145)
  - No public SOC 2 evidence was found for either vendor in this pass.
- **UK NHS gate:** digital therapies can be used only once they have DTAC (Digital Technology Assessment Criteria) approval and an NHS Talking Therapies digitally-enabled-therapy assessment. — [NICE HTE9, anxiety EVA](https://nice.org.uk/guidance/hte9/resources/digitally-enabled-therapies-for-adults-with-anxiety-disorders-early-value-assessment-pdf-1396179075013)
- **Evidence bar:** NICE found "not enough clinical evidence" on Wysa, Iona Mind and Minddistrict to model them economically, and recommended them for research use only (depression, HTE8). — [NICE HTE8 committee discussion](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/HTE8/chapter/3-committee-discussion)
- **Buyers' guide example:** Wysa published a 2023 buyers' guide to mental-healthcare benefits (vendor material, not fetched). — [Wysa 2023 Buyers Guide (PDF)](https://blogs.wysa.io/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2023-Buyers-Guide-to-Mental-Healthcare-Benefits-for-Employees-2.pdf)

### Inferences
- For Aurelia, the minimum credible kit for a first employer pilot is:
  - a security and privacy pack (GDPR or, in Indonesia, PDP-law compliance; a data-processing agreement; an ISO 27001 or SOC 2 roadmap);
  - an aggregate, anonymised engagement dashboard;
  - a crisis-escalation policy.
- A content-only wellness product can avoid HIPAA "covered entity" obligations in direct employer deals. Health-plan deals pull HIPAA in. This is an inference; confirm with counsel.
- "ROI evidence" in practice starts as engagement and self-reported outcomes from a pilot, not a randomised controlled trial (RCT). Clinical claims would push Aurelia toward regulated medical-device territory (DiGA, FDA), which it is not ready for.

### Gaps
- No primary source quantifies what share of employer RFPs require SOC 2 Type II, as opposed to ISO 27001.
- Integration requirements were not found in a sourced form. These include SSO/SCIM, eligibility files, HRIS (e.g. Workday) and EAP hand-off.
- Single sign-on, eligibility-file and reporting-API expectations remain to be researched.

## 4. Insurers and payers: bundling, reimbursement models, evidence required

### Takeaway
US payers mostly bundle wellness apps as no-cost "value-adds" outside the insured benefit, which is a fixed-fee B2B deal, not reimbursement. Kaiser states explicitly that Calm and Headspace "are not covered under your health plan benefits" and may be discontinued at any time. True reimbursement exists in Germany's DiGA scheme, but only for certified medical devices with evidence. Most DiGAs need longer than the one-year trial period to prove a positive care effect, and negotiated prices are roughly halved.

### Cited Findings
- **Kaiser Permanente:**
  - Members get Calm and Headspace at no added cost via kp.org/selfcareapps.
  - Eligible members can text a Headspace coach for 90 days a year.
  - The apps are for ages 13 and over. They are not available to fee-for-service Medicaid or dental-only members.
  - The apps "are not covered under your health plan benefits… and may be discontinued at any time." Current pages. — [Kaiser Permanente, Self-care apps](https://healthy.kaiserpermanente.org/health-wellness/mental-health/tools-resources/digital); [KP Maryland/Virginia/DC page](https://healthy.kaiserpermanente.org/maryland-virginia-washington-dc/health-wellness/mental-health/tools-resources/digital)
- **UnitedHealthcare:**
  - Offered Calm Health to more than 13M commercial members (2024 report, as summarised in search; original article not fetched). — [search summary; see Calm Health press coverage](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/calm-expands-mental-health-support-app-internationally)
  - Earlier offered Sanvello at no extra cost as part of behavioural-health benefits (2021 member handout; may be out of date). — [Arkansas behavioural-health document listing](https://sas.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/SPRJ65778_Behavioral-Health_FINAL-v2.pdf) (surfaced in search; the Sanvello/UHC handout is the underlying source)
- **Headspace and Medicare:**
  - Not enrolled as a direct Medicare or Medicaid provider, but in-network with select Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans. — [Headspace help center](https://help.headspace.com/hc/en-us/articles/37974782380827-Can-I-use-Headspace-therapy-if-I-have-Medicare-or-Medicaid)
  - Launched insurance-covered therapy direct to consumers (June 2025), with typical copays of $0–35 per session. — [HIT Consultant, 2025-06-18](https://hitconsultant.net/2025/06/18/headspace-launches-insurance-covered-therapy-direct-to-consumers)
  - "Headspace for Cigna Healthcare" launched in November 2025. — [HIT Consultant, 2025-11-10](https://hitconsultant.net/2025/11/10/headspace-for-cigna-healthcare-enhances-everyday-mental-health-support)
  - Vitality and Headspace expanded a global partnership for employers and health plans (2024-05-06). — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240506066695/en/Vitality-Headspace-Expand-Global-Partnership-Employers-Health)
- **Calm Health:**
  - Launched October 2022 for payers, providers and self-insured employers. — [TechCrunch, 2022-10-18](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/18/calm-launches-clinical-mental-health-offering-calm-health/); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221018005092/en)
  - Joined the Solera benefits network. — [Calm blog](https://www.calm.com/blog/calm-health-solera-press)
  - Expanded to the UK and Canada. — [Calm Health blog](https://health.calm.com/resources/blog/calm-health-expands-globally-starting-with-uk-and-canada/)
- **Germany, DiGA (statutory reimbursement):**
  - By the end of 2025, 58 DiGA were in the BfArM directory: 48 permanent and 10 provisional.
  - About 1.6M were dispensed by the end of 2025, with about €400M in spending.
  - 16 DiGA (22%) moved from trial to permanent listing. — [GKV-Spitzenverband DiGA report 2025 (PDF)](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/krankenversicherung_1/telematik/digitales/2025_DiGA_Bericht_GKV_SV.pdf)
  - Earlier snapshot (2025-06-16): 44 permanent and 14 provisional. — [npj Digital Medicine 2025](https://link.springer.com/10.1038/s41746-025-01879-6)
  - AOK counts about 1.9M prescriptions since 2020, of which 82% (1.6M) were actually activated. — [AOK, Daten und Analysen](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/daten-und-analysen/digitale-gesundheitsanwendungen/)
  - **Prices:** the 2024 average was €541 per prescription (range €119–€2,077, usually per 90 days). After negotiation or arbitration, prices fall about 50%, to a median of €221. A majority of DiGAs overrun the one-year trial window. — [JMIR 2024;e59013](https://www.jmir.org/2024/1/e59013)
  - **Payer pushback:** GKV-SV (2025-04-02) complains of "rising costs with little benefit" and systematically inflated first-year prices. TK calls for better evidence and cost control. — [GKV-SV press release 2025-04-02](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/gkv_spitzenverband/presse/pressemitteilungen_und_statements/pressemitteilung_2011904.jsp); [TK, Fünf Jahre App auf Rezept](https://www.tk.de/presse/themen/digitale-gesundheit/digitaler-fortschritt/fuenf-jahre-app-auf-rezept-2204274); [GKV-SV press release 2026-04-08](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/gkv_spitzenverband/presse/pressemitteilungen_und_statements/pressemitteilung_2239872.jsp)
- **US prescription digital therapeutics:**
  - Medicare does not cover PDTs, and commercial payers and Medicaid hesitate. Claims were adjudicated case by case.
  - This was a central cause of Pear's failure (see section 6). — [In Vivo / Citeline, Pear bankruptcy and reimbursement](https://invivo.pharmaintelligence.informa.com/IV147722/Pear-Bankruptcy-Filing-Highlights-Reimbursement-Barriers-for-Digital-Therapeutics)

### Inferences
- The payer "bundle" for a wellness app is a B2B licence the plan buys as a member-retention perk. It is not reimbursement, and it is cancellable ("may be discontinued at any time").
- The payers that bundle (Kaiser, UHC, Cigna) bought category leaders with tens of millions of users. A pre-revenue product is not a candidate for that channel in the near term.
- DiGA-style reimbursement requires CE/MDR medical-device certification, a clinical study and a German launch. That path is incompatible with Aurelia's current wellness, non-clinical positioning.

### Gaps
- No primary source was found on what evidence Kaiser, UHC or Cigna required before bundling Calm or Headspace.
- The UHC/Calm Health 13M figure needs its primary article.
- Medicare Advantage supplemental-benefit rules for wellness apps were not researched.
- Sanvello's current status was not verified.

## 5. Health systems and clinics: prescribing and social prescribing

### Takeaway
In the UK, the NHS's route for mental-health apps runs through NICE early value assessments and NHS Talking Therapies. Even well-known apps (Wysa) are rated "research only" for lack of evidence. In the US, Calm Health is sold to "providers" as well as payers. In this pass no sourced evidence was found of health systems paying for general wellness apps at scale.

### Cited Findings
- **NICE HTE8 (depression) and HTE9 (anxiety):**
  - Wysa, Iona Mind and Minddistrict should be used only in research, citing limited evidence.
  - Use also requires DTAC plus NHS Talking Therapies assessment.
  - Deprexis was no longer available in the UK as of January 2025. — [NICE HTE8](https://nice.org.uk/guidance/hte8/chapter/2-The-technology); [NICE HTE8 committee discussion](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/HTE8/chapter/3-committee-discussion); [NICE HTE9 PDF](https://nice.org.uk/guidance/hte9/resources/digitally-enabled-therapies-for-adults-with-anxiety-disorders-early-value-assessment-pdf-1396179075013)
- **NICE rapid healthtech guidance (children and young people):** recommended four digital tools for mild-to-moderate anxiety or low mood, conditional on DTAC. — [NICE news](https://nice.org.uk/news/article/digital-mental-health-tech-for-children-and-young-people-recommended-by-nice-in-first-rapid-healthtech-guidance)
- **Calm Health and LifeStance:** partnered to route users with complex needs to clinical care, a provider-referral model. — [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/calm-health-lifestance-health-partner-users-complex-mental-health-needs)
- **Calm Health positioning:** designed to "bridge mental and physical health care" for providers and payers (2022). — [Healthcare Dive, interview with Calm Health CEO David Ko](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/david-ko-ceo-calm-health-meditation-mental-health-clinical/634622/)

### Inferences
- Health-system and NHS channels demand clinical evidence and assurance work (DTAC, NICE) that cost time and money. They are a stage-3 channel for Aurelia at best.
- "Social prescribing" of non-clinical wellness apps is plausible as unpaid recommendation, not as paid procurement.

### Gaps
- Sleepio's NICE status was not retrieved.
- No sourced data was found on UK social prescribing of apps, or on US health-system wellness-app procurement and pricing.
- The NHS Apps Library / ORCHA-style accreditation routes were not researched.

## 6. Why incumbents struggled: lessons for a small entrant

### Takeaway
The category's leaders have been repriced sharply:
- Headspace merged with Ginger at a $3B valuation in 2021 and is being sold to Sword Health for up to $300M in 2026, after three rounds of layoffs.
- Calm's estimated revenue fell from about $450M ARR (2021) to about $300M, and it pivoted into Calm Health and B2B2C.
- Pear Therapeutics went from a $1.6B valuation to a $6.05M asset sale because payers would not reimburse.

The lesson is that consumer-subscription growth stalled and enterprise buyers consolidated. Reimbursement without payer-accepted evidence and long runway is fatal.

### Cited Findings
- **Headspace:**
  - Merged with Ginger in October 2021 at about a $3B valuation, with more than 800 staff. — [Wikipedia, Headspace (company)](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Headspace_(company)); [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/headspace-health-confirms-second-round-layoffs-less-year)
  - Laid off about 50 staff in December 2022. — [Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/digital-health/headspace-lays-off-about-50-employees/)
  - Laid off 181 staff (about 15%) in mid-2023, including therapists. — [Becker's](https://beckershospitalreview.com/digital-health/headspace-lays-off-181-employees-in-2nd-round-of-job-cuts.html); [HLTH, 2023-07-04](https://hlth.com/insights/news/virtual-mental-health-provider-headspace-health-lays-off-180-employees-2023-07-04)
  - Cut about 13% of staff in November 2024 and moved staff therapists to contract and part-time roles effective 2025-03-15. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/headspace-cuts-13--of-its-workforce--transitions-staff-therapists-contract-part-time-roles)
  - Took $105M in financing to expand the enterprise business (employers and health plans). — [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/digital-health/headspace-banks-105m-new-financing-it-eyes-potential-ma-and-expansion-enterprise)
  - Trade press reports fading consumer interest in mindfulness. — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/388064/as-mindfulness-fades-headspaces-new-marketing-ch.html)
  - **Sword Health deal (2026):** a definitive agreement to acquire Headspace (parent OrangeDot), all-cash, "up to $300M." Axios Pro reported $200–300M. Close expected early Q4 2026. The Oregon Health Authority accepted the notice of material change on 2026-08-27. — [HLTH, 2026-09-17](https://hlth.com/insights/news/sword-to-acquire-headspace-in-deal-worth-up-to-300m); [HIT Consultant, 2026-09-16](https://hitconsultant.net/2026/09/16/sword-acquires-headspace-300m-all-cash-ai-care-mental-health-platform/); [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/sword-health-acquire-headspace-all-cash-deal); [Oregon OHA transaction summary](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/HPA/HP/HCMOPageDocs/090-transaction-summary.pdf)

  - From the $3B merger valuation to ≤$300M is a ≥90% write-down. This is computed from the two figures above; the deals are not like-for-like.
- **Calm:**
  - Last disclosed valuation was $2B (Series C, December 2020). — [CB Insights, Calm financials](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/calmcom/financials)
  - Estimated ARR was about $450M in 2021, falling to about $300M in 2022–2023. These are aggregator estimates that conflict on timing. — [Sacra, Calm](https://www.sacra.com/c/calm)
  - Pivoted to B2B2C with Calm Health (October 2022). — [Sacra note, Calm shifts to B2B2C](https://sacra.com/chat/h/e8dd6888-0b84-46e4-8239-f6c7828eb988/); [Chief Healthcare Executive](https://www.chiefhealthcareexecutive.com/view/calm-moves-into-healthcare-and-is-excited-about-its-prospects)
- **Pear Therapeutics:**
  - Filed for bankruptcy in April 2023.
  - Assets were sold to four buyers for a combined $6.05M, against about $32M of debt and a $1.6B valuation two years earlier.
  - Its SPAC raised $175M against a $400M target.
  - Its root problem was payer non-coverage: no Medicare coverage for PDTs, and hesitant commercial payers and Medicaid. — [Axios Pro, 2023-04-10](https://www.axios.com/pro/health-tech-deals/2023/04/10/after-blazing-a-trail-in-digital-therapeutics-pear-goes-bankrupt); [Axios Pro, 2023-05-22](https://www.axios.com/pro/health-tech-deals/2023/05/22/pear-therapeutics-sold-6m-four-parts); [In Vivo / Citeline](https://invivo.pharmaintelligence.informa.com/IV147722/Pear-Bankruptcy-Filing-Highlights-Reimbursement-Barriers-for-Digital-Therapeutics); [PMC analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12314708/)
  - Digital Therapeutics Alliance CEO: "if they had one more year… their runway wasn't long enough." — [Citeline MedTech](https://medtech.citeline.com/MT147939/Pear-Bankruptcy-Filing-Highlights-Difficult-Challenges-Facing-Digital-Therapeutics)
- **Employer spending:** fell about 7% on mental health in 2025 despite higher utilisation (trade press). — [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/benefits/mental-health-utilization-is-up--but-employer-spending-fell-7-in-2025-587992.aspx)

### Inferences
- **Lessons for Aurelia:**
  1. Do not build a model that needs reimbursement before payer-grade evidence exists (Pear).
  2. Avoid fixed clinical-labour costs before revenue (Headspace's therapist layoffs).
  3. Large buyers are consolidating toward platforms that bundle coaching and care. A content-only app is a point solution at risk of being "replaced," so it should either sell cheaply and self-serve, or partner into a platform or EAP as content.
  4. Activation and retention data from a real pilot are the currency for every channel.
- Sword buying Headspace for its "AI care" positioning suggests acquirers value AI-personalised mental-health delivery. This is weak evidence that Aurelia's AI-generated session angle is strategically relevant.

### Gaps
- Calm's current revenue and valuation are undisclosed, and the figures above are estimates only.
- Headspace's 2025–2026 layoff counts are unverified. A Glassdoor review and InterviewPal mention further cuts with no reliable number.
- The Headspace acquisitions of Sayana and Shine surfaced in search with ambiguous dates. They appear to be 2022, not 2025, and are not central here.

## 7. Southeast Asia and Indonesia

### Takeaway
Indonesian mental-health startups such as Riliv run a B2C app alongside a corporate programme ("Riliv for Company"), priced by subscription or by session. Public pricing and client lists are absent, and regional survey data show weak employer provision: in Singapore, 45% of workers report no EAP. Data specific to Indonesia were thin in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- **Riliv:**
  - A Surabaya-based app, founded 2015 by Audrey Maximillian Herli and Audy Christopher Herli.
  - Offers online counselling with psychologists plus meditation.
  - Runs a B2C model plus B2B "Riliv for Company" for employee mental health. — [DailySocial](https://en.dailysocial.id/post/the-role-of-Indonesian-mental-health-service-startups); [Yahoo/Tech in Asia](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/surabaya-based-startup-tackling-depression-providing-easier-access-051828795.html); [ASEAN Magazine](https://theaseanmagazine.asean.org/?p=1698)
  - Seed round led by East Ventures (announced January 2022). — [East Ventures, Riliv](https://east.vc/portfolio/riliv)
  - Claims more than 500,000 users (vendor claim). — [Riliv App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/riliv/id1510486066)
  - Employer pricing is listed as "employer subscription; session-based" by a third-party EAP directory. — [Meditopia for Work, Top EAP providers in Indonesia](https://meditopia.com/en/forwork/articles/top-eap-providers-in-indonesia)
- **Intellect × Milieu Insight survey:** 6,000 employees across six SEA markets (SG, TH, MY, ID, VN, PH), 1,000 per country, fieldwork 2024-12-23 to 2025-01-08. 56% of Singapore employees say their employer does not support their mental wellbeing well. — [Intellect press release](https://intellect.co/read/intellectxmilieuinsightreport_pressrelease/)
- **TELUS Health Mental Health Index, Singapore (September 2025):** 45% of workers say their employer offers no EAP, and a further 30% are unsure. — [TELUS Health Singapore MHI PDF](https://go.telushealth.com/hubfs/MHI%202025/Q4/Singapore%20MHI%20September%202025.pdf)
- **People Matters:** 1 in 5 Southeast Asian workers see a lack of mental-health benefits as a deal-breaker in a job offer. — [People Matters SEA](https://sea.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/recruitment/1-in-5-workers-in-southeast-asia-view-lack-of-mental-health-benefits-as-deal-breaker-in-a-job-offer-45008)
- **Fortune survey of Southeast Asia business leaders on wellness at work (2026-04-08), not fetched:** — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/08/southeast-asia-business-leaders-wellness-at-work-results/)
- **Marsh Health on Demand 2021 (pre-2022, older):** Indonesian employees with access to 10 or more employer well-being resources were 29 percentage points less likely to move employer. — [Marsh Indonesia](https://www.marsh.com/id/en/services/employee-health-benefits/insights/health-on-demand-2021.html)
- **Intellect** (Singapore-based, regional employer mental-health vendor) cites a 28% app adoption at McDonald's Singapore (vendor claim). — [Intellect](https://intellect.co/read/workplace-wellbeing-in-singapore-asean/)

### Inferences
- In SEA, an employer channel exists but is under-penetrated. Low EAP coverage means less entrenched incumbents, but budgets are also smaller.
- A low per-seat price in local currency, paired with counselling-partner hand-offs (the Riliv model), looks like the regional template.
- Aurelia's Indonesian-language sessions could differentiate it against English-first global vendors. This is an inference; not tested.

### Gaps
- Ruang Jiwa and Halodoc mental-health services were not researched in this pass.
- No Indonesian employer-benefit penetration data or local price points (IDR per employee) were found.
- Indonesian regulatory requirements were not covered: the PDP Law (UU 27/2022) and Ministry of Health rules for digital health.
- Per-country Indonesia figures from the Intellect/Milieu study were not retrieved.
