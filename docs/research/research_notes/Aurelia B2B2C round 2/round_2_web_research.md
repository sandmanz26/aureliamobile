# B2B2C round 2: web research notes

Researched 25 Nov 2026 by web search, as of late 2026. Each finding has its link, the date of the source, and a label: **measured** (a count anyone can repeat), **company** (the company's own page or press release), **third-party** (analysts, press, aggregators), **estimate** (a market-research or our own calculation). Where a search summary was all we had, it says so.

## 1. Employer and health-plan moves by the big apps

- **Headspace is being sold.** Sword Health agreed to buy Headspace in an all-cash deal of up to $300M, closing expected at the start of Q4 2026; closing not confirmed in what we found. Third-party. https://cryptobriefing.com/sword-health-acquires-headspace/ (2026)
- **Headspace via Cigna.** From 1 Jan 2026, "Headspace for Cigna Healthcare" is offered to more than 7 million people through their employers' Cigna plans. Company. https://newsroom.cigna.com/2025-11-10-Headspace-for-Cigna-Healthcare-Enhances-Everyday-Mental-Health-Support-Through-Self-Guided,-Science-Backed-Resources (10 Nov 2025)
- **Calm Health in the Solera Network** (Jan 2026); Calm says Calm Health reaches 39M+ people through plans, providers and employers. Company. https://www.calm.com/blog/calm-health-solera-press
- **Calm team plans.** Groups of 5 to 300, discount grows with size, price via a calculator. Company. https://support.calm.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038432493 ; Calm's own team page says 5–100: https://business.calm.com/team-plan/ (read 25 Nov 2026)
- **What Calm buyers pay.** Vendr: median $7,880 a year over 51 purchases. Third-party. https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/calm
- **Wysa for Teams.** $50 per employee per year, teams of 200 or fewer, unique access code per person. Company. https://wysa.com/for-teams
- **Headspace small business.** Buy seats online; one enrolment link to share. Company. https://organizations.headspace.com/small-business

**Reading.** The large players are moving up-market into clinical care and health plans. The small-team, card-paid, self-serve tier is still sold by all three, at about $50–70 a seat a year. That is the gap a cheap Aurelia team plan sits in, without clinical claims.

## 2. Employer wellbeing budgets, 2026

- WTW (Oct 2026): only 16% of US employers postponing or reducing wellbeing spend; the shift is from more programmes to measured outcomes. Third-party. https://www.wtwco.com/en-us/insights/2026/10/employee-wellbeing-enters-its-next-chapter-smarter-investments-greater-impact
- Wellable 2026: 66% plan to invest more in stress management and resilience (70% in 2025). Third-party. https://www.wellable.co/resources/employee-well-being-industry-trends-report/2026/
- NFP (2025 data): average employer spend on mental-health resources down about 7% year on year. Third-party, via Insurance Business. https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/benefits/nearly-half-of-us-employers-expect-healthcare-budget-increases--nfp-562773.aspx
- Mercer (2025): more employers likely to reduce benefits in 2026 to control health costs. Third-party, search summary. https://mercer.com/en-us/insights/us-health-news/as-benefit-costs-surge-employers-face-tough-decisions-for-2026

**Reading.** Budgets exist but are judged on use. Price low, report activation, never individuals.

## 3. Indonesia and Southeast Asia

- Mercer Marsh Benefits, Indonesia health and benefits study 2025 (400 organisations, 500,000 employees): 48% of companies include an employee assistance programme. Third-party. https://www.marsh.com/id/en/services/employee-health-benefits/insights/id-indonesia-health-and-benefits-study-2025.html
- Market size, corporate wellness Indonesia: IMARC about $968M in 2025, 5.58% a year to 2034. Estimate. https://www.imarcgroup.com/indonesia-corporate-wellness-market . Another firm says $1.9B (platforms). Estimates disagree by about 2×; do not quote either as fact.
- Local players: Riliv (corporate wellbeing and EAP), Mindtera. Search summary. https://meditopia.com/en/forwork/articles/top-eap-providers-in-indonesia
- Content commerce was 32% of Southeast Asia's e-commerce GMV in 2025 (Momentum Works, via Jakarta Post, 27 Jun 2026; paywalled, summary only). Third-party. https://thejakartapost.com/business/2026/06/27/businesses-tap-tiktoks-creator-economy-to-boost-sales
- Tokopedia × TikTok Shop Creators Lab, Bali (Jun 2025): creators trained to earn commission per sale from short videos and live streams. Company. https://www.tokopedia.com/blog/creators-lab-bali-eng/
- No independent count of Indonesian meditation or yoga creators exists; influencer rate pages (Heepsy, Elev8or) are vendor estimates. Gap.

**Reading.** In Indonesia creators already sell through commission, which fits a creator share. Employers have assistance programmes; Aurelia goes beside them, in Bahasa, priced in rupiah.

## 5. Trust patterns for creator-sold programmes

- Maven Guarantee: full refund until the course midpoint, shown as a badge. Company. https://help.maven.com/en/articles/8705540-maven-s-satisfaction-guarantee
- Maven refunds return the expert's share and Maven's fee; Stripe fees are not refunded. Company. https://help.maven.com/en/articles/5597391-issuing-refunds-to-students
- Free trial length: trials of 17–32 days convert 42.5% against 25.5% under 4 days; hard paywall 10.7% against freemium 2.1% of downloads by day 35. Measured, 115,000+ apps. https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026
- Health & Fitness trial-to-paid 35.0%, highest category. Measured, 16,000 apps. https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-2026
- FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials, in force 21 Oct 2024: bans fake and AI-generated reviews and testimonials and fake social-media indicators; civil penalties per violation (launch figure $51,744). Company (regulator). https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials
- Nielsen: 83% globally trust recommendations from friends and family (2021 data). Third-party. https://www.nielsen.com/global-trust-in-advertising
- BBB National Programs Influencer Trust Index (Feb 2025): 26% do not trust influencers; the report's own toplines conflict (87% vs 74%). Third-party. https://bbbprograms.org/media/insights/blog/influencer-trust-index

**Reading.** The working pattern is: free first, a visible refund window to the midpoint, the creator's own voice, and only real, consented testimonials.

## 6. Creator dashboards
