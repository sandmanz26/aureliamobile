# Insight Timer: product feature set (as of November 2026)

Researched 2026-11-15 from public sources only: the App Store listing, the Insight Timer help center (insight-timer.freshdesk.com, which help.insighttimer.com points to), press releases and one app-analytics article. The Google Play page did not render for the fetch tool, and two press-release URLs returned 403/404, so Android-specific facts are thin. "Last updated" stamps quoted below are the help-center article stamps, which date the text, not the feature.

## Library: size, content types, organisation, offline

### Takeaway
The free library is claimed at 300,000+ practices across guided meditation, sleep, music, talks, podcasts, yoga/movement and live video. The App Store release notes report about 900-980 new free meditations and about 470-520 new free sleep/music/talk/podcast tracks per release, with releases roughly weekly. Bookmarks, playlists and recently-played are free. Downloads for offline use are a MemberPlus feature.

### Cited Findings
- The free tier has "over 300,000 guided meditation, sleep, breathing, and mindfulness practices", plus sleep music and ambient soundscapes, live meditation and yoga classes, "hundreds of free courses", a "meditation timer with custom settings", and breathing and body-scan practices. (Article last updated 2026-03-19.) — [Help: What free features does Insight Timer offer?](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664765-what-free-features-does-insight-timer-offer-)
- The App Store description says "Thousands of guided meditations and mindfulness practices" and "100+ new free guided practices added daily". It names 18 teachers (e.g. Tara Brach, Jack Kornfield, Sadhguru) "and many more". Release notes cite 3 million monthly active members. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/insight-timer-meditation-app/id337472899)
- Release cadence: 25 versions from 20.19.0 (May 28) to 20.42.1 (Nov 6, 2026). Each note follows one template: "X new free meditations" (913-981) and "Y new free sleep tracks, music, talks and podcasts" (468-517), plus "UI improvements". The notes give no feature-level detail. — [App Store version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/insight-timer-meditation-app/id337472899)
- The March 2026 India launch was described as giving access to "more than 300,000 guided meditations, music tracks and talks". — [The Wire / PTI press release](https://m.thewire.in/article/ptiprnews/worlds-largest-free-wellness-app-insight-timer-launches-in-india/amp) (summarised via search result, not fetched in full)
- The help center's "Library" and "My Bookmarks, Playlists, and Downloads" sections document these user features: recently played tracks, keep-screen-awake, using the Timer offline, manually adding a session, bookmarking a track, finding bookmarks, creating playlists, adding tracks to a playlist, and removing unavailable content from bookmarks. — [Help center: Community category](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/67000385167)
- Teachers can publish guided meditations, talks, music, podcasts and other audio, as free or premium tracks. — [Help: Free vs Premium track](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000715474-what-is-the-difference-between-a-free-and-premium-track-)
- Offline listening (downloading practices) is listed as a MemberPlus benefit. (Article last updated 2026-10-21.) — [Help: What is MemberPlus?](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664702-what-is-memberplus-)
- Platforms: iPhone (iOS 15+), iPad, Mac (Apple silicon, macOS 12+), Apple Vision (visionOS 1+) and Apple Watch (watchOS 10+). App size is 242.8 MB. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/insight-timer-meditation-app/id337472899)
- There is an LG TV app; the help center has a folder on its features, installation, guest use, and whether other TVs are supported. — [Help center: About the LG TV App folder](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/67000385167)
- The app can be used without an account in "Guest Mode". There are articles on what happens when a guest registers or signs into an existing account. — [Help center: Getting Started folder](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/67000385167)

### Inferences
- "300,000+" (help center) and "thousands" (App Store) are not in conflict; the store copy is understated. 300k is the number to use.
- Taken literally, ~1,400 new free tracks per weekly release is roughly 70k a year. That fits a library of hundreds of thousands built mostly from user-generated (teacher-uploaded) content, not commissioned content.

## Courses, Challenges, Live events, Groups/community

### Takeaway
Courses are now MemberPlus-only (unlimited access with the subscription), not bought one by one. Each course has a teacher-moderated classroom with audio replies. Live events are real-time video (meditation, yoga, talks, breathwork) found under a "What's On" tab, funded by donations. The App Store does list a $3.49 "Live Event Ticket" purchase, which conflicts with an older "all live events are free" statement. Past daily Challenges are a MemberPlus archive. Groups, group meditations, profiles, blocking and an opt-out for social features remain.

### Cited Findings
- Courses: "organized learning paths" led by teachers, done at your own pace "with daily or sequential lessons". Each has a teacher-moderated classroom where teachers reply to selected questions "with real-time audio". "Courses are available only to MemberPlus subscribers, who get unlimited access to all available courses." (Article last updated 2026-03-16.) — [Help: What are Courses](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664709-what-are-courses-and-how-do-they-work-)
- Conflict: the free-features article (2026-03-19) also claims "hundreds of free courses". — [Help: free features](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664765-what-free-features-does-insight-timer-offer-)
- MemberPlus includes "multi-session video courses" and "video-guided tracks". — [Help: What is MemberPlus?](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664702-what-is-memberplus-)
- Course tooling for teachers: audio course requirements, video introductions for audio and video courses, engaging course students, attaching a PDF to a course, and a course survey. — [Help center: Teachers category](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/67000385221)
- Challenges: MemberPlus gives "Challenges anytime: access to the full archive of past daily challenges". — [Help: What is MemberPlus?](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664702-what-is-memberplus-)
- Live events are "real-time sessions where you can stream guided meditations, yoga and movement classes, talks, breathwork". They appear in "Live Now" and a Live Events feed under the **What's On** tab. Signing up for one teacher's event subscribes you to notifications for all of that teacher's sessions. Example formats are recurring morning meditations, weekly sleep stories, sunrise yoga, live music and breathwork. ## Teacher/creator platform and earnings

### Takeaway
Anyone can apply to publish through a teacher account (profile requirements, credentials encouraged, review before publishing) and manage everything in a Teacher Dashboard. Earnings come from a share of MemberPlus revenue, weighted by content type and by a "Return Rate" bonus introduced mid-2024 that takes 25-30% of teacher income. Donations go 100% to the teacher, less Apple's 30% on iOS. Retreats and (apparently) paid live events add further income.

### Cited Findings
- Subscription share is based on "play count, content type, and listener engagement". Premium Tracks are weighted above Free Tracks; Free Tracks, Premium Tracks, Courses, Free Videos, Premium Videos and Premium Video Courses are each weighted differently, but no percentages are published. A play counts after 120 seconds (tracks under 2 minutes must be played in full). — [Help: How can teachers earn revenue](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664874-how-can-teachers-earn-revenue-from-their-work-) (last updated 2026-04-22)
- Donation presets are US$2.99, $7.99 and $30.99, with custom amounts up to $1,000. Teachers receive 100% with no Insight Timer fee; Apple takes 30% on iOS. Payouts go out on the 21st of each month via Tipalti, with a $10 minimum that rolls over, and earnings unclaimed after 12 months are donated to charity. — [same](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664874-how-can-teachers-earn-revenue-from-their-work-)
- Conflict: the App Store lists donation purchases at $1.99, $4.99, $19.99 and $49.99. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/insight-timer-meditation-app/id337472899)
- The Return Rate bonus was announced 27 June 2024. It measures the share of a track's listeners who come back the next day and complete an activity. The fund is "25% to 30% of all teacher income". Tracks are compared within Guided, Sleep, Music, Kids and Non-English, with free and paid grouped separately, and earn Excellent/Great/Good tiers. A US$550,000 Teacher Assistance Fund covered July-September 2024 income shortfalls. — [Help: Teacher Income FAQ August 2024](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000737721-teacher-income-faq-august-2024)
- Premium tracks are MemberPlus-only, need studio-quality audio and high-resolution art, and their payouts are "significantly higher" than free ones. Free tracks build the audience. — [Help: Free vs Premium track](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000715474-what-is-the-difference-between-a-free-and-premium-track-)
- Publishing premium content and live events (beyond live yoga) requires 2 published free tracks first. — search summary of help-center content ([Help: What kind of content does Insight Timer accept](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664794-what-kind-of-content-does-insight-timer-accept-)); not fetched in full.
- Creator tooling covered in the help center: teacher account creation and profile requirements, accreditations, upload specs, scheduled release and series publishing, review and rejection process, the dashboard and stats over time, embedding content on external sites, serialized content guidance, video guidelines (recording, camera, lighting), trailer videos, retreat listing, Teacher Terms, and trust and safety (spam in live events, harassment). External promotion of outside services is banned. — [Help center: Teachers category](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/67000385221)
- AI policy for teachers: "We do not allow AI generated content on Insight Timer." AI is allowed for brainstorming, outlines and sentence editing, and for audio cleanup. Fully AI-written scripts, AI voices and voice clones, AI-only music, and AI images are prohibited. All content is reviewed before publication. (Last updated 2026-11-12.) — [Help: Can teachers use AI](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000723124-can-teachers-use-artificial-intelligence-ai-on-their-insight-timer-offerings-)
- Video guidelines prohibit AI narration voices and allow AI music only as background under a human-led track. — [Help: Video guidelines](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000750966-video-guidelines-for-teachers) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- The Return Rate bonus pays teachers for app retention, not raw plays. It is a notable, transferable mechanism.
- The donation price difference ($2.99/7.99/30.99 versus the App Store's $1.99-$49.99) probably reflects web/Android versus iOS price points, or a list that is out of date.

### Gaps
- A current teacher count was not found (only the 18 named on the App Store).
- Course revenue share and paid live-ticket split were not published.
- The "Teacher Hub" branding was not confirmed; the help center calls it the "Teacher Dashboard".

## Premium / MemberPlus: price, inclusions, changes

### Takeaway
MemberPlus costs US$9.99 a month or US$59.99 a year, with prices varying by region. It unlocks courses, premium audio and video, offline downloads, advanced player controls (repeat, fast-forward/rewind, resume), the challenge archive, custom folders and milestones, an advanced journal and premium timer sounds. A trial is offered (7 days on annual per third parties). There is no family plan now, after a trial of one ended.

### Cited Findings
- The full MemberPlus inclusion list and prices as above, billed via iTunes/Google Play or the web, with auto-renew. "A free trial may be offered" (length not stated). Last updated 2026-10-21. — [Help: What is MemberPlus?](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664702-what-is-memberplus-)
- The free-features article lists MemberPlus as offering "ad-free listening, offline downloads, premium teacher content, and advanced courses". — [Help: free features](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664765-what-free-features-does-insight-timer-offer-)
- "Advanced player controls: repeat mode, fast forward and rewind, and resuming where you left off" sit behind MemberPlus, which means rewind and resume are paywalled. — [Help: What is MemberPlus?](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664702-what-is-memberplus-)
- Family plan: "a limited family plan trial has ended and the feature is no longer available". Last updated 2026-03-19. — [Help: Family plan](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664700-does-insight-timer-offer-a-family-plan-for-memberplus-). The App Store still lists "MemberPlus Family $89.99". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/insight-timer-meditation-app/id337472899)
- App Store purchases: "Insight Premium Meditation" at $59.99 and $9.99, and "Live Event Ticket" at $3.49. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/insight-timer-meditation-app/id337472899)
- A 7-day free trial for annual subscribers, then $59.99 a year; prices confirmed as of 2026-10-19. — [RecurDash](https://recurdash.com/subscription-pricing/insight-timer) (third party)

## Integrations, personalisation, AI, gamification, 2025-2026 changes

### Takeaway
Apple Watch is supported (watchOS 10+): sessions sync to the iPhone and it can control playback. In January 2026 the company launched New Year's resolution and daily intention features driven by an "AI recommendation engine", and cited 32M+ members. A May 2026 redesign (v20.17.0) moved bookmarks and drew backlash. Journal, check-ins, milestones, stats and session-log export make up the progress layer.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Watch help articles: fixing watch sessions that don't sync to the iPhone, and controlling iPhone audio from the watch. — [Help center: Apple Watch folder](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/67000385167)
- Sessions completed on the website count toward statistics; journal entries and session logs can be exported. — [Help center: My Journey folder](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/67000385167); [Help: export session logs](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000739180-how-can-i-export-my-session-logs-on-insight-timer-)
- The advanced journal (quotes, links to practices, daily check-ins, deeper prompts) and custom milestones are MemberPlus. — [Help: What is MemberPlus?](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664702-what-is-memberplus-)
- January 6, 2026: resolutions shown on the home screen all year as a daily reminder, with prompts to set smaller daily intentions, powered by an "AI recommendation engine", on iOS and Android, from a company with "over 32 million members". — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/06/3213643/0/en/insight-timer-launches-new-year-s-resolution-and-intention-setting-features-with-ai-recommendation-engine.html); [Insight Timer blog copy](https://insighttimer.com/blog/?p=13268) (both summarised from search; not fetched in full)
- v20.17.0 (May 13, 2026) was an unannounced redesign that moved bookmarks into the new interface. Its US review average fell from 4.23 to 3.14 (small samples: 30 and 72 reviews). A Reddit thread from 20 May 2026 is titled "User Interface is Unusable". — [MWM](https://mwm.ai/articles/app-updates/insight-timer-v20-17-0-buries-saved-meditations-in-bloated-redesign-may-2026)
- The navigation tabs referenced in 2026 help text are What's On, Saved and Home (the Saved tab is customisable via Edit). — [Help: join live events](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000665057-how-can-i-join-and-watch-live-events-on-insight-timer-)
