# Insight Timer (Insight Network Inc.): company strategy, business model and market position, to November 2026

Research date: 2026-11-15. Public sources only. Many figures are company claims or third-party estimates; each is labelled. Several primary pages (GlobeNewswire, Business Wire) returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so some details come from syndicated copies or search snippets.

## 1. History, founders, leadership, HQ, team size

### Takeaway
Insight Timer launched in 2009 as a meditation timer founded by Brad Fullmer. Brothers Christopher and Nicho Plowman (Australian) later bought it and turned it into a teacher-content platform. Christopher Plowman is still CEO in November 2026. Press releases carry a San Francisco dateline, and historically most staff were in Australia, with offshore teams in Manila and Surat, India. Headcount has been reported at roughly 80 to 120 since 2020.

### Cited Findings
- Insight Timer launched in 2009. Plowman calls it "the first app in the App Store" (a loose claim). The original founder was Brad Fullmer, who brought in long-time meditators ("the elders") who set the community's tone. Plowman says he did not start the app: he and his brother Nicho bought it from another owner and changed it significantly. Bo Shao is chairman and helped set up the company's foundation. — [John Koetsier, 16 Nov 2020 (companion to a Forbes piece)](https://johnkoetsier.com/no-selling-allowed-how-insight-timer-is-building-the-biggest-meditation-community-on-the-planet/)
- The company's About page says it "started as a meditation timer in 2009". The legal entity is Insight Network Inc. — [insighttimer.com/about](https://insighttimer.com/about)
- Crunchbase lists Christopher Plowman as CEO and co-founder, with Nicho Plowman as co-founder. Crunchbase is a secondary aggregator. — [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/insight-network-inc); [Crunchbase person](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/christopher-plowman)
- Plowman is quoted as CEO in the January 2026 release ([Yahoo Finance / GlobeNewswire, 6 Jan 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/insight-timer-launches-resolution-intention-133700660.html)) and in the American Counseling Association's July 2026 announcement ([Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/american-counseling-association-adds-insight-timer-to-prestigious-strategic-partner-network)). A November 2026 YouTube talk at Transform Trauma Oxford also bills him as CEO ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XHFU8Cpmu98)). No leadership change was found.
- HQ: the January 2026 release is datelined San Francisco, and its press line is a +1 628 (San Francisco) number. — [Yahoo Finance / GlobeNewswire](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/insight-timer-launches-resolution-intention-133700660.html)
- Team in 2020: "about 120 staff", with about 70 in Australia, about 40 in Manila and about 40 in Surat. Those parts sum to about 150, so the article is internally inconsistent. Plowman worked from Bali. — [Koetsier 2020](https://johnkoetsier.com/no-selling-allowed-how-insight-timer-is-building-the-biggest-meditation-community-on-the-planet/)
- Team in February 2024: about 100 employees. — [TechCrunch, 21 Feb 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/21/in-a-reversal-apple-is-now-demanding-30-of-the-donations-to-meditation-app-insight-timers-teachers/)

### Inferences
- Headcount fell from about 120 (2020) to about 100 (2024) to about 80 (2025). That fits the 2024 push to profitability described in section 3.
- The San Francisco dateline probably reflects a US corporate and marketing presence. The engineering and operations base has historically been Australia plus offshore teams. That split is inferred, not confirmed for 2026.

### Gaps
- No primary source gives the exact year the Plowmans acquired the app or what they paid. Secondary sites commonly say the mid-2010s, but I found none credible enough to cite.
- No 2026 headcount or office list, and no current executive team beyond the CEO. Craft.co lists executives but was not verified: [craft.co](https://craft.co/insight-timer/executives).

## 2. Business model: free library, MemberPlus subscription, teacher revenue share, donations, courses, live, retreats, B2B

### Takeaway
The model is "free library, no ads, one subscription". A free catalogue of 300,000+ tracks drives reach. Revenue comes mainly from the MemberPlus (Premium) subscription, which unlocks courses, offline listening and similar features, and a large share of it is paid out to teachers. Teacher donations, a retreats marketplace that takes about 10%, and live events add to that. The clinician channel (Therapist Hub, ACA partnership) is free, and it reads as a distribution play rather than a paid B2B line.

### Cited Findings
**Subscription**
- In 2020 the app was free, with no ads and no selling to users. A paid subscription covered long-form courses and one-to-one teacher time, and under 1% of users subscribed. Each subscription dollar split roughly a third each to Apple, teachers and Insight Timer. — [Koetsier 2020](https://johnkoetsier.com/no-selling-allowed-how-insight-timer-is-building-the-biggest-meditation-community-on-the-planet/)
- Premium adds offline listening, journaling and unlimited course access. Subscriptions brought in about $20 million in 2023, and Apple took 30% of that. Insight Timer shares 50% of subscription revenue with teachers. — [TechCrunch, Feb 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/21/in-a-reversal-apple-is-now-demanding-30-of-the-donations-to-meditation-app-insight-timers-teachers/)
- Price points found: Premium $59.99 a year, per a University of Georgia library guide of unknown date ([UGA LibGuide](https://libguides.law.uga.edu/Mindfulness/insight-timer)), and a MemberPlus Family in-app price of $89.99 in the US App Store ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/insight-timer-meditation-timer/id337472899)). These come from search snippets. Current prices vary by market and are A/B tested (next point).
- Most new users now see a paywall during onboarding, and others see it when they tap gated content. Prices are tested regularly: in one market (the UK was mentioned), a roughly 30% price cut reportedly raised revenue about 50%. Apple billing makes price tests hard. — [Lou Redmond podcast, Mar 2025](https://louredmond.com/podcast-1/2025/03/11/insight-timer-ceo-christopher-plowman-on-teacher-earnings-how-the-platform-is-changing)

**Teacher revenue share**
- In about June/July 2024 the teacher commission was cut from 50% to 40%, adding about $150,000 to the bottom line. Plowman says payouts are "gradually getting back to the original payment". Some teachers saw income fall up to 50%, which he attributes to the commission cut, a new "return rate" ranking algorithm and rising play counts that dilute the per-play rate. Subscription revenue is paid out as 1/12 per month over 12 months. Teachers must not "off-ramp" subscribers to sell outside the platform. — [Lou Redmond podcast, Mar 2025](https://louredmond.com/podcast-1/2025/03/11/insight-timer-ceo-christopher-plowman-on-teacher-earnings-how-the-platform-is-changing). The year 2024 is inferred: the transcript says only "June/July".
- Conflict: aggregator sites still quote a 50% share ([Accio](https://www.accio.com/biz-company/insight-timer)), while one says the company turned profitable in 2024 after cutting it to 40% (search-snippet summary of [businessmodelcanvastemplate.com](https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/blogs/how-it-works/insight-timer-how-it-works)). The CEO's own account (40% in 2024, rising again by 2025) is the best source.

**Donations and the Apple dispute**
- Users tip teachers through a donation feature processed by Stripe, and Insight Timer takes no cut. Donations ran at about $100,000 a month. Apple's 2022 rule 3.2.1 allowed commission-free gifts when 100% goes to the recipient, and Apple approved 47 updates over 12 months. In late 2023 a reviewer reclassified donations as "digital content". The compromise left profile-page donations commission-free, while tips during live events and meditations became commissionable and could not link to the profile page. Plowman complied by the February 2024 deadline: "I don't want to pick a fight with Apple." — [TechCrunch, 21 Feb 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/21/in-a-reversal-apple-is-now-demanding-30-of-the-donations-to-meditation-app-insight-timers-teachers/)

**Retreats, live and marketplace**
- January 2025 retreat sales were about $100,000. Insight Timer kept about $10,000 (roughly a 10% take) and paid about $90,000 to organisers. Plowman called it "day one for retreats", with no browsable directory yet. A Teacher tab replacing the Community tab was planned, to list retreats and live events. Under consideration, with no dates: teacher marketplace sales, paid challenges, paid live events. Direct-to-teacher subscriptions were deferred because of Apple's 30% fee. — [Lou Redmond podcast, Mar 2025](https://louredmond.com/podcast-1/2025/03/11/insight-timer-ceo-christopher-plowman-on-teacher-earnings-how-the-platform-is-changing)
- Live streaming is expensive. The company considered shutting it off but kept it as a sales tool, with prompts during broadcasts to buy courses or retreats. About 10% of users take part in lives, per the CTO. — same source

**Clinicians / B2B**
- In October 2025 Insight Timer launched a free Therapist Hub for mental health professionals. — [GlobeNewswire, 8 Oct 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/08/3163166/0/en/Insight-Timer-Launches-Free-Therapist-Hub-to-Support-Mental-Health-Professionals.html) (title and date from search; the page body returned 403)
- The About page claims 125,000 therapists "provide clinical insight into what's helping their clients". — [insighttimer.com/about](https://insighttimer.com/about); see also [insighttimer.com/therapists](https://insighttimer.com/therapists)
- In July 2026 the American Counseling Association added Insight Timer to its Strategic Partner Network. — [Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/american-counseling-association-adds-insight-timer-to-prestigious-strategic-partner-network)

### Inferences
- The free clinician tools look like a referral funnel: therapists send clients to the free app, and some of those clients convert to MemberPlus. No source shows a paid enterprise or clinician product.
- Revenue grew from donation-friendly community monetisation toward a harder paywall during onboarding, plus transaction take-rates (retreats about 10%). The pressures behind that were Apple's commission and the push to profitability.

### Gaps
- No primary source for 2026 MemberPlus pricing, or for any workplace / employer plan in 2025-2026.
- No figures for course sales separate from the subscription, or for live-event revenue.

## 4. Strategy shifts 2023-2026: video, live, health/HRV, Map of Healing, therapists, AI, monetisation, onboarding, new markets

### Takeaway
From 2024 to 2026 the strategy has been to monetise harder and stay "human". Changes include a paywall during onboarding, retreats and live as sales channels, and a "return-rate" ranking that rewards content people come back to. The company has repositioned around a "Map of Healing" and clinicians (125k therapists claimed, Therapist Hub, ACA partnership). It also expanded to India in March 2026. On AI, it markets "No AI. Humans heal humans", while shipping AI recommendation ("Reflect", the intention "digital librarian") and AI moderation.

### Cited Findings
- Return-rate ranking: in 2024-25 it replaced plain play counts, to favour content that brings listeners back. — [Lou Redmond podcast](https://louredmond.com/podcast-1/2025/03/11/insight-timer-ceo-christopher-plowman-on-teacher-earnings-how-the-platform-is-changing)
- Map of Healing: the About page centres on a "Map of Healing" / "World's Map of Wellbeing" that maps practices by mechanism and effect on the body. The mission line is "Healing is a human right", with free access to everyone on earth, and the company calls itself "a healthy business without harmful incentives". — [insighttimer.com/about](https://insighttimer.com/about)
- AI stance:
  - The About page says "No AI." and "Humans heal humans, not machines", but also that AI helps analyse large data sets ([About](https://insighttimer.com/about)).
  - The teacher-recruitment page advertises "free, no ads, no AI" ([Join as teacher](https://insighttimer.com/meditation-teachers/join)). The therapist page says every practice is taught by a real person ([Therapists](https://insighttimer.com/therapists)).
  - Plowman (2025): the company will not create fake AI teachers, and AI may help teachers manage more student relationships ([Lou Redmond](https://louredmond.com/podcast-1/2025/03/11/insight-timer-ceo-christopher-plowman-on-teacher-earnings-how-the-platform-is-changing)).
  - January 2026: proprietary AI reads each user's daily intention and acts as a "digital librarian" recommending content. "Reflect" is named as Insight Timer's AI, with an AI safety page at insighttimer.com/ai-safety ([Yahoo / GlobeNewswire](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/insight-timer-launches-resolution-intention-133700660.html)).
  - Google Cloud Vision is used to detect spam in the community ([Google Cloud case study](https://cloud.google.com/customers/insight-timer)).
  - A third-party 2026 guide classes Insight Timer as using AI "to recommend, not generate" ([wellness-ai.app](https://www.wellness-ai.app/guides/best-ai-meditation-apps-2026)).
- Goals and onboarding: in January 2026 the app launched a New Year's Resolution and Intention-Setting feature. The resolution sits on the home screen all year, with daily intention prompts and shareable lock-screen graphics. A 31-day Intention Setting Challenge featured Jack Kornfield, Nedra Glover Tawwab, Lalah Delia and Alex Elle. — [Yahoo / GlobeNewswire](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/insight-timer-launches-resolution-intention-133700660.html)
- Monetisation: paywall in onboarding for most new users; retreats marketplace (about 10% take) launched about January 2025; Teacher tab replacing Community; paid challenges and paid lives under consideration; live kept as a sales tool despite its cost. — [Lou Redmond podcast](https://louredmond.com/podcast-1/2025/03/11/insight-timer-ceo-christopher-plowman-on-teacher-earnings-how-the-platform-is-changing)
- Clinicians: free Therapist Hub (October 2025) ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/08/3163166/0/en/Insight-Timer-Launches-Free-Therapist-Hub-to-Support-Mental-Health-Professionals.html)); ACA Strategic Partner Network (July 2026) ([Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/american-counseling-association-adds-insight-timer-to-prestigious-strategic-partner-network)); Plowman talk at Transform Trauma Oxford (November 2026) ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XHFU8Cpmu98)).
- New markets: official India launch on 10-11 March 2026, featuring pranayama, mantra chanting, chakra sessions, sound healing and Indian teachers. — [The Print / ANI](https://theprint.in/ani-press-releases/worlds-largest-free-wellness-app-insight-timer-launches-in-india/2875253/); [The Wire](https://m.thewire.in/article/ptiprnews/worlds-largest-free-wellness-app-insight-timer-launches-in-india)

### Inferences
- The "No AI" message differentiates Insight Timer from generative-AI meditation apps, and it protects the teacher supply that is its moat. It refers to content, not to the absence of AI recommendation or moderation. The two positions are reconcilable but open to accusations of inconsistency.
- The clinician and trauma framing (Map of Healing, therapists, ACA, Transform Trauma) suggests a move from "meditation app" toward "evidence-mapped mental-health library". That could set up future B2B or clinical revenue, but none is announced.

### Gaps
- No primary source found for video features, health or HRV integration, or wearables in 2023-2026. The brief mentions these, but I could not confirm them within the search budget.
- No evidence of gamification beyond intentions and streak-like challenges, and no specifics on language expansion beyond the "50 languages" claim.

## 5. Market position versus Calm, Headspace and others; market size and retention

### Takeaway
Insight Timer is the scale leader in free usage and teacher supply, with low revenue. Calm and Headspace are subscription-revenue leaders that are shrinking. Calm had an estimated $210M in 2025 revenue (-24%) and about 3.5M subscribers. Headspace had an estimated $140M and about 2M subscribers, and was sold to Sword Health in 2026 for up to $300M, against a $3B peak valuation. Insight Timer has roughly a tenth of Calm's revenue, but it is profitable on about $30-40M raised versus hundreds of millions for its rivals. Market-size estimates vary by a factor of 50 and are unreliable.

### Cited Findings
- Calm: estimated 2025 revenue $210M, down 24% year on year; about 3.5M subscribers (500k fewer than 2024); 7.3M downloads in 2025; CEO David Ko. These are estimates. — [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/calm-statistics/). Calm raised its annual subscription price in February 2026, new price not given ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/calm/)). Conflict: GetLatka gives Calm $596.4M revenue, apparently a 2024 company-reported figure ([GetLatka](https://getlatka.com/blog/calm-revenue)). Calm previously laid off about 20% of staff ([MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/meditation-app-calm-lays-20-workforce)).
- Headspace: estimated 2025 revenue $140M and about 2M paid subscribers (down 300k) ([Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/headspace-statistics/)). Lifetime in-app revenue about $476M through April 2026, with a peak year of $71M in 2022 (estimate) ([Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/headspace)).
- Headspace sale: Sword Health agreed an all-cash deal worth up to $300M ([pharmaphorum](https://pharmaphorum.com/news/sword-health-buys-headspace-200m-300m-deal); [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/sword-health-acquire-headspace-all-cash-deal); [HLTH](https://hlth.com/insights/news/sword-to-acquire-headspace-in-deal-worth-up-to-300m)). It closed in about late September / early October 2026 and was followed by layoffs of undisclosed size, with CEO Tom Pickett retained. Before the cuts the US headcount was Headspace 579 plus Sword 624 ([Behavioral Health Business, 16 Oct 2026](https://bhbusiness.com/2026/10/16/headspace-issues-layoffs-following-sword-health-acquisition/)). Headspace was valued at about $3B after the 2021 Ginger merger and had raised about $320M ([Trending Topics](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/headspace-once-worth-3-billion-sells-for-300-million-plus-9-more-ma-deals/)). Some sources date the signing to August, others to September 2026.

### Inferences
- Calm and Headspace combined had an estimated $350M in 2025 revenue, falling. On a ~$20-30M base, Insight Timer is a small revenue player but plausibly the largest by free audience and catalogue. Its claim to beat all wellbeing apps combined on time spent is plausible, given the long timer sessions (24-minute average in 2020), but unverified.
- Headspace's distressed sale and Calm's decline support Insight Timer's lean, low-capital, profitable model as the more durable one in a saturated, low-retention category. They also show how weak valuations are across the category.
