# Creators, live events and retreats (raw notes, fetched 25 Nov 2026)

Labels: [company] = Insight Timer's own page; [third-party]; [estimate] = ours.

## How teachers earn
- [company] Subscription share: MemberPlus revenue shared "based on play count and content type"; premium tracks weighted above free; courses and video count; listener engagement counts. No fixed percentage published. https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664874-how-can-teachers-earn-revenue-from-their-work- (25 Nov 2026)
- [company] A play counts only after 120 seconds (or the full track if under 2 min). Paid monthly on the 21st via Tipalti; under US$10 carries forward; unclaimed after 12 months goes to charity. Same page.
- [company] Return-rate fund: 25–30% of all teacher income. Return = listener back the next day doing anything (play, course, journal, live, timer, check-in). Scored per category (Guided, Sleep, Music, Kids, Non-English), free and paid separately; above-average tracks earn a bonus in tiers Excellent/Great/Good. Tip given to teachers: "Create tracks for daily practice". https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000748946-understanding-return-rates (25 Nov 2026)
- [company] Donations: presets, teacher keeps 100%; Apple takes 30% on iOS. Same earnings page. App Store lists donation purchases $1.99–$49.99. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/insight-timer-meditate-sleep/id337472899 (25 Nov 2026)
- [third-party] "roughly 50% of subscription revenue" to creators: not on any company page, unconfirmed. https://www.starterstory.com/businesses/insight-timer/monetization
- [company, press release] More than 20,000 teachers; 300,000+ tracks; 35M+ users (10 Mar 2026, India launch). https://theprint.in/ani-press-releases/worlds-largest-free-wellness-app-insight-timer-launches-in-india/2875253
- [third-party, competitor-authored] Mindful Leader, 17 Nov 2026: the return-rate bonus as a possible conflict with students' long-term interests; the CEO quoted (Mar 2025): "We needed people to come back to our platform more frequently"; an 83-day course among the biggest earners. The author runs a competing app (disclosed). https://www.mindfulleader.org/blog/125585-is-insight-timer-one-of-the-good-guys

## Live events
- [company] Real-time streams (meditation, yoga, talks, breathwork, sound baths) in a "What's On" tab; teachers earn donations and promote tracks, courses and retreats from them. https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000754308-what-are-live-events-
- [company] App Store purchase "Live Event Ticket" $13.99 (US); ₹349 / ₹1,499 (India). Paid lives exist.

## Retreats (their paid "Retreats" product)
- [company] Listing is free. On booking, Insight Timer keeps a non-refundable deposit of 10% of the total; the teacher collects the other 90% directly from the participant by a balance date they choose (0–150 days before). https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000739470-insight-timer-retreats-faqs-for-teachers (25 Nov 2026)
- [company] Minimum price: multi-day US$200, one-day US$100, "to offset fixed startup costs", to be lowered later. Same page.
- [company] Insight Timer promotes retreats on the Teachers tab, through in-app notifications to followers, on post-meditation screens, on the retreats website and the teacher profile. Same page.
- [company, via search summary of the help centre] At launch: in-person multi-day retreats; teachers had to show they had hosted one before. https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000739960-your-step-by-step-guide-to-listing-a-retreat-on-insight-timer
- [company] Example listings: Spirit Rock "Opening to Insight and Love", 11–17 Dec 2026, US$1,470 a person (US$960 shared). https://insighttimer.com/retreat/opening-to-insight-and-love ; a one-day Napa yoga and hiking retreat for 15 people, "10% Deposit to Reserve", "Best Price Guaranteed", host joined Nov 2025. https://insighttimer.com/retreat/adventure-2026-retreat
- Not found: any evidence that a retreat continues in the app afterwards (a course or journey for attendees). Absence of evidence, not proof.
