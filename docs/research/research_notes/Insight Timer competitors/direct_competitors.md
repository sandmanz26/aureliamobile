# Insight Timer's direct competitors in meditation, sleep and mental wellness (as of 16 November 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-16. Public sources only. US App Store listings were fetched on 2026-11-16 and are the primary source for prices; "US" means the US storefront. This file builds on `../Insight Timer deep research/strategy_business.md` (Calm and Headspace revenue estimates, the Sword–Headspace deal) and `ai_landscape_aurelia_fit.md` (Headspace Ebb, Calm Sleep, Endel, Rosebud, Woebot). Those findings are only summarised here, with their original links, and are not re-researched.

**Insight Timer baseline used for comparison** (all from the earlier notes):
- MemberPlus costs US$9.99 a month or $59.99 a year — [Insight Timer Help](https://insight-timer.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/67000664702-what-is-memberplus-), updated 2026-10-21.
- The company claims 300,000+ free tracks and 32M+ members — [GlobeNewswire via Manila Times, 2026-01-06](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/01/06/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/insight-timer-launches-new-years-resolution-and-intention-setting-features-with-ai-recommendation-engine/2253735).
- Subscription revenue was about $20M in 2023 — [TechCrunch, Feb 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/21/in-a-reversal-apple-is-now-demanding-30-of-the-donations-to-meditation-app-insight-timers-teachers/).

## 1. What each app is, who it is for, core loop and flagship features

### Takeaway
The field splits into four groups:
- **Broad paid libraries:** Calm and Headspace.
- **Single-voice or curated "depth" apps:** Waking Up, Happier and Open.
- **Personalised-session apps:** Balance.
- **Free nonprofit apps:** Medito, Smiling Mind, Plum Village and Healthy Minds.

BetterSleep is sleep-first. Hallow and Finch are adjacent apps of similar commercial weight. Insight Timer is the only one built as an open teacher marketplace.

### Cited Findings
- **Calm.** Subtitle "Sleep, Meditation, Relaxation"; tagline "Calm is the #1 app for sleep, meditation, and relaxation". Flagship feature: "100+ exclusive Sleep Stories, new content added every month", plus daily programs, 7- and 21-day programs, and sleep music and soundscapes. Rated 4.8 from 2M ratings; Editors' Choice — [Calm, US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/calm/id571800810) (fetched 2026-11-16).
- **Calm Sleep.** A separate iOS app launched 2025-09-16. It builds a personalised sleep plan of recommended content and daily tasks — [TechCrunch, 2025-09-16](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/16/calm-launches-standalone-ios-app-for-sleep-support); [Calm press release](https://blog.calm.com/blog/calm-sleep-press-release).
- **Headspace.** "Your expert-led guide to mental health, mindfulness, and meditation"; category line "Mental Health, Stress, Anxiety". It bundles 500+ guided meditations, sleepcasts, Ebb (an "empathetic AI companion"), and licensed therapists and mental-health coaches. Rated 4.8 from 974K ratings; seller Headspace Inc. — [Headspace, US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/headspace-meditation-sleep/id493145008) (fetched 2026-11-16).
- **Waking Up.** "Waking Up: Meditation & Wisdom", subtitle "Insights for a Better Life". Created by Sam Harris. Core loop: a 28-day Introductory Course, then a daily meditation, plus theory sessions, life courses, conversations and Q&As, Sleep, a timer and CarPlay. Techniques include Vipassana, loving-kindness, yoga nidra and nondual practice. Rated 4.9 from 43K ratings. Badges: "2025 Pick by NYT Wirecutter" and 2026 App of the Day. Seller Waking Up Course, LLC — [Waking Up, US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/waking-up-meditation-wisdom/id1307736395) (fetched 2026-11-16).
- **Happier (formerly Ten Percent Happier).** Listed as "Happier Meditation", subtitle "Practice Healthy Mindfulness": "Meditation for stress, anxiety, and sleep — that meets you where you are." Rated 4.8 from 143K ratings; seller Happier Meditation, Inc. — [Happier Meditation, US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id992210239) (fetched 2026-11-16).
- **10% with Dan Harris.** The original founder's new app (Dec 2025) "brings together meditation and community in one simple place" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/10-with-dan-harris/id6755701096); [Wikipedia: Dan Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Harris_(journalist)).
- **Balance.** Subtitle "Breathe, calm anxiety & stress". Positioned as a "personal meditation coach" that assembles a daily guided session "from an audio library of thousands of files", tailored to mood and goals, and says personalisation improves as users share more. Rated 4.9 from 121K ratings. Seller Elevate Labs, LLC; developer The Mind Company — [Balance, US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1361356590) (fetched 2026-11-16).
- **Open.** "A Mindfulness Studio" whose stated aim is to "regulate your nervous system". It offers on-demand breathwork (from 5 minutes), daily meditations, monthly programs and movement classes of up to 60 minutes, has an in-person studio in Los Angeles, and integrates with Oura. Rated 4.9 from about 18K ratings; Editors' Choice; seller Open Inc. — [Open, US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/open-breathwork-meditation/id1482725254) (fetched 2026-11-16).
- **Medito.** "The best free meditation app with no hidden costs." It offers guided meditations, beginner courses, 30-day challenges, sleep stories and sounds, breathing exercises, a timer and daily meditations. No account and no ads — [Medito Foundation](https://meditofoundation.org/free-meditation-app/) (undated page, fetched 2026-11-16).
- **Smiling Mind.** "Your daily workout for life", a "mental fitness toolkit" for all ages. It covers adults, families (including a parenting pathway), children and a schools program. Features: Routine (a custom plan with reminders) and a Fitness tab that tracks skills, frequency and mood — [Smiling Mind app page](https://smilingmind.com.au/smiling-mind-app) (fetched 2026-11-16).
- **Plum Village.** A free Zen app from Thich Nhat Hanh's tradition. It has 100+ guided meditations, 300+ video sessions and Q&As with Thich Nhat Hanh and Plum Village teachers, a customisable timer and a "Mindfulness Bell". Offline downloads; 7 languages. Developer: Centre for Applied Ethics, described as a "non-profit gift" — [Plum Village, US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1273719339); [plumvillage.app/about-the-app](https://plumvillage.app/about-the-app/) (as summarised by search, 2026-11-16).
- **BetterSleep (formerly Relax Melodies).** Sleep sounds and mixes, bedtime stories and sleep tracking. Developer Ipnos Software; seller BetterSleep Health, LLC — [BetterSleep, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id314498713). Relax Melodies was renamed BetterSleep on 2021-11-30, after Teladoc acquired Ipnos — [GlobeNewswire, 2021-11-30](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2021/11/30/2343201/0/en/Relax-Melodies-rebrands-to-BetterSleep-on-its-path-to-become-the-leader-in-digital-sleep.html).
- **Healthy Minds Program (Healthy Minds Innovations, a nonprofit).** Free, no ads, "up to 600 days of free content" — [Healthy Minds Program, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1326310617); [hminnovations.org](https://hminnovations.org/?p=6858) (as summarised by search).
- **Other apps of similar commercial weight.** Hallow, a Catholic prayer and meditation app, has raised about $105M. Finch, a self-care pet app, is estimated at about $1M a month in revenue. Both are covered in sections 3 and 6.

### Inferences
- Positioning lines cluster around outcomes: Calm on sleep, Headspace on "mental health", Open on "nervous system", Balance on "coach". Waking Up ("wisdom", "insights") and Plum Village (tradition) sell depth instead. Insight Timer's "largest free library" is the only line built on breadth plus price.
- Headspace is now positioned as a mental-health service (therapists, coaches, AI companion), not a meditation library, so it competes with Insight Timer less directly than Calm does.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch the Google Play listings, so Android positioning and prices are unverified.
- I did not fetch Healthy Minds' or Plum Village's own pages directly. The Plum Village "major upgrade" press release returned HTTP 503.

## 2. Price in 2026 (US) and what the free tier includes

### Takeaway
Paid apps fall into two bands:
- **About $70 a year:** Calm, Headspace, Balance. The monthly price is $11.99–$16.99.
- **$100–$150 a year:** Happier ($99.99), Open ($149.99) and Waking Up ($149.99, with a $199.99 SKU also listed).

Insight Timer's $59.99 a year is the cheapest paid tier, and its free tier is by far the largest. Medito, Smiling Mind, Plum Village and Healthy Minds have no paid tier at all.

### Inferences
- **Ranking by annual price (US):** Waking Up and Open ($149.99) > Happier ($99.99) > Calm, Headspace and Balance ($69.99) > Insight Timer ($59.99) > the free nonprofits ($0).
- Insight Timer undercuts every paid peer and gives away far more. Its real price competitors are the free nonprofits, Medito above all, which have no paywall drift.
- The premium "depth" apps (Waking Up, Open) charge more than twice Insight Timer's price. That suggests a single trusted voice or a studio experience is worth more to its buyers than catalogue size is.

### Gaps
- No primary (company web page) prices were fetched for any app. All prices come from App Store in-app purchase lists, which do not label periods. Web checkout prices (for example Calm's $79.99 on the web) can differ.
- I could not determine Calm's February 2026 new price.

## 3. Scale: users, subscribers, revenue, funding and ownership

### Takeaway
Only Calm and Headspace are well above Insight Timer in revenue: Calm about $210M (estimated, 2025) and Headspace about $140M (estimated). Waking Up is the next largest paid app, at perhaps $8–9M a year from iOS alone (estimate). Most other players are privately held, report little and are small. Ownership:
- Headspace now belongs to Sword Health.
- BetterSleep belongs to Teladoc.
- Medito, Smiling Mind, Plum Village and Healthy Minds are nonprofits.

### Inferences

- Insight Timer is plausibly the third-largest pure meditation business by revenue and the largest by free audience. Waking Up is the closest premium challenger.
- Valuations across the category have collapsed (Headspace went from $3B to about $0.3B), and funding has stalled: Open's last round was in 2021, Happier's in 2018. Insight Timer's profitability on modest capital (about $30–40M raised) is unusual in the category.

## 4. Content model and library size

### Takeaway
Insight Timer is the only open marketplace of teachers, with 300,000+ tracks from about 20,000 teachers. Every other app is curated:
- **In-house plus celebrity:** Calm (about 100+ Sleep Stories).
- **In-house, clinical framing:** Headspace (500+ meditations).
- **Founder voice plus a named roster:** Waking Up, Plum Village, Dan Harris's new app.
- **Studio instructors:** Open.
- **Algorithmically assembled from recorded fragments:** Balance.

Medito and Smiling Mind are small curated libraries (Smiling Mind about 700 sessions).

### Cited Findings
- **Calm:** "100+ exclusive Sleep Stories, new content added every month" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/calm/id571800810). The standalone Calm Sleep app advertises 500+ Sleep Stories — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/calm/). Calm has moved into video with an HBO Max series — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/calm/). Sleep Stories remain human or celebrity narrated — [ai_landscape notes](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3045822).

## 5. Personalisation and AI features

### Takeaway
No major competitor generates guided audio with AI. AI is used for:
- **Chat:** Headspace's Ebb, Insight Timer's Reflect.
- **Recommendations and plans:** Calm search and Calm Sleep plans, Insight Timer intentions.
- **Fragment assembly:** Balance, pre-LLM style personalisation.

App Store listings for Waking Up, Happier, Open, Medito, Smiling Mind and Plum Village mention no AI. Headspace under Sword is the competitor most likely to push AI hard.

## 7. How each compares with Insight Timer (free vs paid, human vs AI, breadth vs depth)

### Takeaway
Insight Timer sits in a unique corner: free and very broad, human-made through a marketplace, and cheap at the paid tier. Competitors beat it on:
- **Curation and depth:** Waking Up, Plum Village.
- **Clinical and enterprise reach:** Headspace, Calm Health.
- **Sleep specialism:** Calm, BetterSleep.
- **Per-person session assembly:** Balance.
- **Zero paywall:** Medito, Smiling Mind.

The clearest gap no one fills is a session generated for one person's moment, which Insight Timer has chosen not to build.

### Inferences
- **Free vs paid.** Insight Timer wins against every paid app except on curation. Against Medito, Smiling Mind and Plum Village it has the much bigger library, but it carries a paywall those apps lack, and gated content draws backlash (see `users_backlash.md`).
- **Human vs AI.** Every major player keeps its content human. Headspace and Insight Timer use AI for conversation, Calm and Insight Timer for discovery, and Balance for assembly. "Human-made" is not a differentiator against Calm, Waking Up or Plum Village. It differentiates only against generated-content entrants.
- **Breadth vs depth.** Insight Timer is the breadth extreme. Waking Up and Plum Village are the depth extreme and command the highest price (Waking Up). The beginner middle ground (Headspace, Calm, Balance) sells a guided path, which is the thing Insight Timer users say they lack.
- **Gaps in the market:**
  1. Truly per-person sessions: Balance comes nearest, and nobody generates them.
  2. Outcome-tracked progress: Smiling Mind's mood tracking and Calm Sleep plans are partial attempts.
  3. Clinical routing for consumers, away from employers: Headspace and Calm Health focus on enterprise.
  4. A billing-trust brand at the $70 price point.
