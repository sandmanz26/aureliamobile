# Ideation: every B2B2C model for Aurelia, scored

*Part of the [B2B2C research](/wiki/b2b2c). The evidence behind each score is on the detail pages; the scores themselves are our judgement, not a benchmark.*

## In one minute

- B2B2C means someone who already has the customer brings Aurelia to them: a creator, an employer, a partner. The question is never only "which channel". It is **who pays to acquire the user, and who owns the card afterwards**.
- We listed 15 models and scored each on six questions. The four that score best share two traits: the partner pays for acquisition, and the end user ends up paying Aurelia directly.
- **Highest scores:** trial codes and gifting (16), a redesigned creator cohort (15, not the spec as written), retreat and studio aftercare (14), then the self-serve team tier for small employers (13).
- **Lowest:** reimbursed clinical routes (6), insurers (7), enterprise employer contracts (9), and free whole-base perks and universities (10). They need clinical evidence, a sales team or a wholesale price Aurelia cannot afford yet.
- Eugene's Retreat-in-a-Box as specified scores lower than the same idea redesigned. The idea is right; the €19.99 ticket, the 60/40 split, 24 fully personalised sessions per buyer and a physical box are what break it ([Unit economics](/wiki/b2b2c/economics), [Creators](/wiki/b2b2c/creators)).

## How each model is scored

Each question scores 1 (poor) to 3 (good). The total is out of 18.

| Question | 3 means | 1 means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who pays acquisition** | The partner brings the users at no media cost to Aurelia | Aurelia pays to reach each user |
| **Who owns the card** | The end user pays Aurelia directly, so they can stay after the deal | The partner pays; users vanish when the deal ends |
| **Time to first revenue** | Weeks, with a small team | A year or more, procurement or trials |
| **Build needed** | Little beyond accounts and payments | A sales team, integrations, certifications |
| **Claims and data risk** | Wellness claims only, Aurelia controls the words | Partners speak for Aurelia in health terms, or clinical evidence is required |
| **Fit with Aurelia** | Uses what is unique: one sentence becomes a personal session, chapters, check-ins | Aurelia would be one more content library |

## The fifteen models

| # | Model | Who pays acquisition | Card | Time | Build | Risk | Fit | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Creator cohort, redesigned**: paid 8-week journey in a creator's style and voice, pre-produced with personal inserts, 70/30 plus a share of the Plus it drives | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | **15** |
| 2 | **Team tier for small employers**: self-serve, about $50–70 a seat a year, aggregate dashboard | 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | **13** |
| 3 | **Retreat and studio aftercare**: "your retreat continues as chapters" for retreat operators, yoga and breathwork studios | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | **14** |
| 4 | **Trial codes and gifting**: hotel stays, brand packs, "gift a journey"; codes convert at retail with a card | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 | **16** |
| 5 | **Wearable integration**: check-ins read sleep and stress from a ring or watch, sold with Aurelia's own subscription (the Oura × Headspace model) | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | **12** |
| 6 | **Carrier billing in Indonesia**: Plus on the phone bill through a telco, revenue share | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 2 | **12** |
| 7 | Creator cohort **as specced**: €19.99, 60/40, 24 personalised sessions, physical box, 76.5% week-8 target | 3 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 3 | **13** (fails on economics, see below) |
| 8 | **Creator channel inside Plus**: creators publish styles and voices, paid by plays, like Insight Timer's teacher pool | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | **13** |
| 9 | Coaches and wellness practitioners using Aurelia between sessions with their clients | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | **12** |
| 10 | Universities, licence while students are enrolled | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | **10** |
| 11 | Boutique gyms and fitness chains as an add-on | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 1 | **12** |
| 12 | Free whole-base telco or bank perk (the SaskTel model) | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 | **10** |
| 13 | Enterprise employer contracts and EAPs | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | **9** |
| 14 | Insurers and health plans as a value-add | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **7** |
| 15 | Reimbursed clinical routes: German DiGA, NHS | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **6** |

**Why model 7 scores 13 but should not be built as written.** Its channel scores are good; its arithmetic is not. Aurelia's 40% of a €19.99 web ticket is about €6.45, while 24 personalised sessions with a premium voice cost about $13 per buyer, so each buyer can lose money before anyone converts to Plus. The split is also far above the 0–10% creators pay course platforms. See [Unit economics](/wiki/b2b2c/economics) and [Creators](/wiki/b2b2c/creators).

**Why trial codes score highest but come second.** They are cheap and low risk, but they bring users one at a time and teach us little about the product. The creator cohort is the bigger bet and the better test of Eugene's model, so it leads the [plan](/wiki/b2b2c/plan), with codes run beside it.

## What the redesign changes in Retreat-in-a-Box

The redesign is of the offer and the business model, not of any screen. The idea stays the same: a creator sells an 8-week bad-to-good journey to their followers, and graduates move to Plus. What changes is the terms.

| Spec | Redesign | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| €19.99 ticket | Test €19.99 against a higher ticket (for example €39) in the buyer agents first | At €19.99 a full cohort earns a creator about €1,936; big creators will not bother, small ones cannot fill 200 seats |
| 60% creator / 40% Aurelia | 70/30, plus a share of the Plus subscriptions the creator's cohort produces | Creators keep 87–97% on course platforms; Insight Timer pays on ongoing plays |
| 24 personalised sessions per buyer | A creator journey produced once, in their voice, with short personal inserts from each check-in | Keeps AI cost under about €2 per buyer |
| Physical box in every ticket | Digital welcome kit; a physical box only as a priced add-on or gift edition | A modest kit costs about $14–28 delivered (fulfilment alone $9–18), most or all of the ticket |
| 76.5% active at week 8, 30% of graduates to Plus | Plan on 20–50% active; report Plus per ticket buyer (planning case about 10%) | Mindfulness apps keep 4.7% at day 30; cohorts do better, but not 16 times better |
| "+85.7% sleep", "stress −50%" | No numbers until we measure our own; wellness wording only | They come from mock data; a creator's post can make the app a medical device |

## Ideas we considered and dropped

- **Selling the AI engine white-label to other apps.** That is B2B, not B2B2C. It gives away the brand and the card, and the margin is voice cost.
- **Ads inside free sessions.** It contradicts the calm product and puts ad SDKs next to health data, the exact pattern behind the BetterHelp, GoodRx and Cerebral fines ([Claims and regulation](/wiki/b2b2c/regulation)).
- **Employer mood dashboards per person.** Since February 2025 the EU AI Act bans AI that infers emotions at work. Employers get aggregate numbers with minimum group sizes, never individuals.
