# Roast: what a sceptic attacks, and what changed

*Part of the [B2B2C research](/wiki/b2b2c). After the pages were written, we read them as four hard readers would: Eugene, a sceptical investor, a creator deciding whether to sign, and a regulator. Each finding below either changed the pages or is listed as a limit that stays.*

## In one minute

- **Fixed:** nine problems, from numbers that disagreed between pages to a summary that claimed more sources than it showed.
- **Still open:** the research cannot answer the plan's key number, Plus per ticket buyer, because nobody publishes it. Only a pilot will.
- **The biggest risk in the plan is not in the research.** It is whether 50 creators exist who can each fill a cohort; that is why creator interviews come before any build.

## Limits that stay

1. **No public data on paid programmes converting to a subscription.** The 30% per graduate is plausible against trial-to-paid benchmarks, but it is not evidence. It is the pilot's job.
2. **No public data on how many followers buy.** The 10,000–40,000 engaged email subscribers needed to fill 200 seats is our estimate.
3. **Partner terms are private.** The [Partners](/wiki/b2b2c/partners) page judges deals by whether they were renewed, not by results.
4. **Several figures are leads, not facts.** They came from search summaries; check each before quoting it outside the team.
5. **Indonesia is thin.** Its data-protection rules, its medical-software decree (KMK 951/2026) and payouts to Indonesian creators need a local check.
6. **One currency shortcut.** The economics treat $1 as €1. This slightly overstates the euro cost of AI.
7. **The legal pages are not legal advice.** They summarise rules as of November 2026, and some (the EU Digital Fairness Act, the AI Act's "omnibus" delay) are proposals, not law.

## What would change the recommendation

- **If creators refuse any split Aurelia can afford,** the retreat stops being the first channel, and the team tier and trial codes lead.
- **If the buyer agents and creators both prefer a higher ticket,** most of the economics problem disappears; test €39 before building.
- **If Aurelia decides to make clinical claims,** every page changes. That means becoming a medical device, which is the road the [Employers and insurers](/wiki/b2b2c/employers) page shows to be slow and costly.
