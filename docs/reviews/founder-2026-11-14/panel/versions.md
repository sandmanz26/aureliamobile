# The three versions you can see

The app comes in three designs. Each is described here, and you can look at real screenshots (open the images with your image-reading tool):

Notes so you do not misread a screenshot: the player shows "Page not found" only because of how the screenshots were taken (in the app it is a full-screen session with one play button). All numbers in the app are sample data.

**Version 1.** A menu behind a ☰ button. Home says "Create the space you imagine", with a box to type how you feel and quick chips (Hard to sleep, Stressed, Anxious). When you sign in, a "Daily check-in" sheet asks how you feel today, whether yesterday's session helped, and for notes. The chat opens on an example conversation that is not yours, then builds your session; a "+" list shows the changes you can apply, with a summary before you apply. Your coin balance shows at the top of most screens. "My Wellness" shows progress as percentages and up/down arrows.

**Version 2.** Everything in Version 1, but: four tabs at the bottom (Create, Discover, Progress, Me) instead of the menu. After your first sign-in, a short welcome and three quick questions (your goal, how you feel, how sessions should sound), each skippable. Your first session is built from your own words, in a conversation of your own. After listening it asks "How do you feel now?" and says chapter 2 is ready tomorrow. Coins only show under Me. Progress still uses percentages.

**Version 3 (the default).** Everything in Version 2, but: progress is told in words from your own check-ins, no percentages, with a short report every two weeks. Every change Aurelia suggests has a "Why?". Onboarding asks which wearable you use (Apple Watch, Whoop, Oura) and leaves it off until you switch it on. An optional private journal. Community shows stories without rankings. Home for visitors says "Sessions made for how you feel today."
